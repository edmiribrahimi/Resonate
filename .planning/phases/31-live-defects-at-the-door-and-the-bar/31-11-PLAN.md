<objective>
Make the door say the same three things with the radio on and with the radio off,
and make the queue visible at the moment it matters.

What the scanner does today:

- **`ScanFlash` has two states**, `"success" | "error"`, hard-coded to
  `bg-green-500/90` and `bg-red-500/90` (`ScanFlash.tsx:5, 28-30`). A repeat is
  therefore red, and FIX-04 is explicit that it must never be a refusal: the person
  in front of the scanner was already admitted.
- **"Not in cache" offline is a red refusal** (`ScannerClient.tsx:389` —
  "Ticket not found (offline)"). That is the false refusal the asymmetry forbids,
  and it is also the case a ticket bought this afternoon lands in.
- **A duplicate offline shows "Connection error"** — the `catch` at `:597-622` is
  one handler covering a duplicate, a 500 and a dead radio, and it ends at `:621`
  with a single message.
- **The pending count is rendered only inside the offline branch** of the status
  pill (`:858-868`; the count is at `:866`). While the device is online — which is
  exactly when a stuck queue matters and exactly when it can be drained — the count
  is invisible. Classifying the queue in plan 31-10 without fixing this surface
  leaves FIX-08 half-done: the number would mean something and nobody would see it.
- **`cacheAttendees(...).catch(() => {})`** (`:170`) means the merge guard's
  refusal has nowhere to surface at all.

Two decisions this plan takes out loud rather than discovering on a night:

1. **The third state is amber, and amber is not `yellow-500`.** `yellow-500` is
   already the Offline pill (`:864-866`). Use the `amber-500` family for the third
   scan state, so a staff member glancing at a phone in the dark does not read one
   as the other.
2. **`ScanFlash` gains a third `type` value, never a colour prop.** The prop is
   semantic. The two `isSuccess ? … : …` ternaries do not extend to three cleanly,
   so both are replaced by one lookup object keyed by `type`. That also leaves
   Phase 42 (DS-04) exactly one place to retint.

Purpose: the door is a safety surface used one-handed, in the dark, with a queue
in front of it. Colour is never the only channel.

Output: `src/components/scanner/ScanFlash.tsx` and
`src/app/(admin)/admin/scanner/ScannerClient.tsx`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| scanned string ↔ the device | Offline the HMAC cannot be checked — the signing secret is server-side only and must stay there |
| local cache ↔ the verdict | Offline, what the device holds is the whole basis of the answer |
| the screen ↔ the only observer | With no error tracking, the person holding the phone is the only place a failure can surface |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-31-11-01 | Spoofing | Offline, a well-formed but forged `uuid.64-hex` token is admitted, because the signature cannot be verified without the server secret | accept | Deliberate, and the requirements already took this decision. The alternative is shipping `TICKET_SIGNING_SECRET` to every staff phone, which would make each one a ticket forge. The window is bounded: the entry is flagged at the door, it lands in the review list as `invalid_signature`, and FIX-11 surfaces it to staff as soon as the network returns. Written into a comment at the branch, not left to be discovered |
| T-31-11-02 | Denial of service (against a paying guest) | A red refusal for a ticket bought after the cache was downloaded, or for a repeat entry | mitigate | Both become amber admissions with a flag; `grep -c "Ticket not found (offline)"` returning 0 is the static assertion |
| T-31-11-03 | Repudiation | A failed sync or a refused refresh invisible to everyone, because no error tracking exists | mitigate | Counters rendered in both connectivity states; the merge refusal rendered as a persistent banner rather than a toast |

| T-31-11-SC | Tampering | npm installs | accept | This plan installs nothing |
