<objective>
Produce the evidence, in the only three forms this project has.

`workflow.verifier` is `true` and this project has already produced seventeen
VERIFICATION documents across thirty phases. This phase must produce one, and it
carries an unusual burden: **there is no test runner for the product.** No `test`
script, no `*.test.*`, no `*.spec.*`. Nothing here may be called verified because
tests pass. `31-VALIDATION.md` names, per requirement, which of three kinds of
evidence applies — a `file:line` assertion, an observable behaviour, or a written
manual procedure — and this plan produces exactly those.

Two requirements need re-assertion rather than implementation. FIX-01 and FIX-02
were applied on 2026-08-05, ahead of the roadmap, in commit `a36b7d9`. They are
genuinely applied: the middleware deletes all three `x-user-*` headers at
`:131-133` **before** setting them at `:136-138`, and both drink callers throw on
the RPC's `false`. They still belong to this phase, so they carry their evidence
into this document rather than being taken on trust from a commit message.

One requirement can only be proved by a negative. FIX-13's "no automatic label is
attached to a member" is not a feature to demonstrate; it is a grep over the files
this phase added or changed, returning zero writes to `public.profiles`, with its
output recorded.

And two things are lost or open, and saying so is part of the work. The refund
rows already destroyed in production cannot be recovered. `Math.random()` in
`src/utils/qr.ts:49`, the absence of rate limiting, and the absence of any error
tracking remain true and remain deferred — by name, not by omission.

Purpose: `meta-gates.md` — in a repository with no tests, written observable
evidence is the only proof that will ever exist. A `31-VERIFICATION.md` without a
single `file:line` citation does not satisfy the gate.

Output: `31-VERIFICATION.md`, and `31-VALIDATION.md` updated with its statuses.
</objective>
