<objective>
Put the door's offline ground in place before anything else in this phase is built
or verified.

Two things are wrong today and both are invisible in `npm run dev`.

1. `src/app/sw.ts:23` adopts `runtimeCaching: defaultCache` wholesale.
   `@serwist/next` 9.5.6's `defaultCache` contains a `NetworkFirst` rule matching
   **every** same-origin `GET /api/*` — `cacheName: "apis"`, `maxEntries: 16`,
   `maxAgeSeconds: 86400`, `networkTimeoutSeconds: 10`. On a weak signal the
   attendee fetch therefore *succeeds* after up to ten seconds with a list that may
   be a day old, while `navigator.onLine` is still `true`, and the scanner writes
   that stale list over the good local cache. That is the operative mechanism
   behind FIX-06. It also means the full member roster
   (`/api/membership/list`) sits in a browser cache bucket on every staff phone
   for 24 hours.

2. `next.config.ts:7` sets `reloadOnOnline: true`. When the signal returns the
   page reloads: the camera stream is torn down, the selected party and the
   in-memory undo list (`ScannerClient.tsx:94`) are gone — with a queue in front of
   the operator and a queue of people in front of the phone.

Purpose: until the cache boundary lands, **every offline verification in this
phase measures the wrong cache and proves nothing**. This plan is first for that
reason, not because it is small.

Output: a route-specific service-worker configuration, a reconsidered reload
setting, and the written runbook `checkin-offline.md` requires before a night.
</objective>
