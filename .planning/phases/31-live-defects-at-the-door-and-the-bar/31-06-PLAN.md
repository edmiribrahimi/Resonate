<objective>
Make the payload the offline door is built from carry the facts the door has to
state.

Three gaps, all in one route.

1. **An Event Pass never reaches the cache.** `tickets.party_id IS NULL` is a real,
   sold product — the partial unique index `tickets_event_user_master_unique`
   (`20260226300000_multi_sub_events.sql:66-68`) exists for it, and the owner has
   confirmed it is issued. But `attendance/route.ts:73` filters tickets with
   `.eq("party_id", party.id)`, so an event-level ticket is never in the attendee
   list and never in the offline cache. The correct predicate already exists eleven
   lines below, in this same file: the guest-list query at `:120` uses
   `.or("party_id.eq.<id>,party_id.is.null")`. Fixing only the check-in route
   (plan 31-07) would produce a fix that works online and fails at the door with no
   network — the two sides change together or not at all.

2. **A refunded ticket is invisible to the payload.** Under Option B the ticket row
   is deleted and the evidence lives on `ticket_refunds`
   (`refunded_ticket_id`, `refunded_party_id`, `refunded_event_id`, `refunded_at`,
   added in plan 31-04). Without those rows in the payload, an offline scan of a
   refunded ticket lands on *not in the cache* — which still admits, correctly, but
   cannot name the holder and cannot flag the reason. With them, the same scan
   resolves as *in the cache, refunded* and produces the amber admit with a reason,
   offline, which is what FIX-09 asks for.

3. **A guest-list entry cannot say when.** `:129` hard-codes `checkedInAt: null`
   because the columns did not exist. They exist now. The third outcome promises
   "who recorded it and when"; showing "Already checked in ·" with nothing after the
   separator is worse than the weaker sentence.

Purpose: the offline verdict is only as good as what was downloaded before the
signal went. Everything the door must be able to say has to be in this payload.

Output: `src/app/api/tickets/attendance/route.ts`.
</objective>
