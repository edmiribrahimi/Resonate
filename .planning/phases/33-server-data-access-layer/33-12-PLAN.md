the marker that matters. Eight of the nine service-client header readers sit
behind a middleware capability gate. This one does not. That is why it is the
probe surface, and it is why it is in this plan and not in the mechanical ones.

member surfaces whose role/status expressions are presentation.

<conversion_contract>
1. **A decisional expression becomes a capability question.** "Decisional" means
   it changes a query, selects a branch that reads with the service client, or
   gates a `redirect` / `notFound`. **A purely presentational expression keeps
   its `role` / `status` form**, now sourced from the session — phase 34
   (STAFF-03) owns the nav and affordance vocabulary, against four roles.
2. **Never a role list.** `staff` arrives one phase later.
3. **`CAP.STAFF_MANAGE` is the key for both `canManage` and `canSeeDrafts`**, and
   the question is recorded: *"may this person manage a staff surface"*.
   `CAP_DESCRIPTIONS["staff.manage"]` names drinks and events by hand, and its
   predicate — role ∈ {master, organizer}, status ignored — is byte-equal to the
   `role === "master" || role === "organizer"` it replaces, so no role's reach
   changes. `ADMIN_ACCESS` would narrow to master; `CATALOGUE_MANAGE` would
   require approved status and refuse a pending organizer who sees these today.
   Both are verdict changes.
4. **No `try`/`catch` around the resolve.**
5. **Delete the `headers` import and orphaned type imports.**
6. These are pages, so `cache()` memoises: several questions in one render cost
   one round trip. Still prefer one destructure.
</conversion_contract>
