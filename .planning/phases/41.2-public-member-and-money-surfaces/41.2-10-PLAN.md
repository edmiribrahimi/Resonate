<objective>
Convert the shared money core — the four files every drink redemption and every
ticket purchase in the product passes through — once, in a wave of its own, ahead
of the three surfaces that reach them.

Purpose: **this is knot 1, and it is Critical rather than cosmetic.**
`/events/[slug]`, `/events/[slug]/menu` and `/dashboard` all reach these files;
two of the four are reached by all three. That is the topology plan 41.1-17 met
and solved by converting the shared file once, as spine, in an earlier wave — so
that the surfaces that use it stay separate plans instead of being merged into
one. Doing it here discharges the constraint for the whole rest of the phase: no
later plan opens any of these four, and each of their SUMMARYs will say so.

them they hold **seven of the tree's sixteen remaining third-breakpoint uses**.
The primitive **deletes** that boundary rather than migrating it, because the
primitive owns the sheet-versus-window pair; migrating it would leave a second
author for a decision one component already makes.

Output: four files, two dialogs off the ratchet, and a money path proved unmoved
line by line.
</objective>

## Written before the diff — required by the Response Gate and by the knot rule

**Classification: Critical.** These four files render the controls through which
money moves: a drink token is redeemed at a bar, and a ticket is bought. The
persona's own rule applies — *measure twice, cut once* — so the approach is
stated here and the diff follows it, not the other way round.

**Domain:** `ticketing-payments` primary; `nextjs-architecture` secondary.
`meta-gates.md`'s monotone guard on a payment reaching completion binds every
line: **a payment's status corrects forward and never backwards**, so nothing here
may make a status appear other than it is.

**What is opened, and it is exactly four files:**
`src/app/(public)/events/[slug]/RedeemConfirmationModal.tsx` (213 lines),
`src/app/(public)/events/[slug]/SumUpCheckoutModal.tsx` (129),
`src/app/(public)/events/[slug]/DrinkTokenCard.tsx` (155),
`src/components/SumUpCardWidget.tsx` (137).

**What is not opened, and must not be.** The redemption confirmation calls a
server action that lives in an **already-converted work module** outside this
perimeter; the checkout path reaches the provider's own SDK. Neither is edited.
Those modules are **read** so that a rendering change can be told from a payload
change, and reading is where that ends. **No server action in this phase's
perimeter carries a class string** — the only file with the server directive among
the fifty-one has zero class attributes — so a conversion here has no reason to
open one, and needing to is stop condition 2.

**Cross-domain impact:**
- *Ticketing and payments:* the confirmation's copy, its focus target and its
  button variants all sit on the path to money moving. Changing any of them
  changes how easy that path is to walk by accident.
- *Check-in and the door:* a drink token redeemed at a bar is a control operated
  at speed, at night, in front of a queue. `checkin-offline.md`'s asymmetry has
  its analogue here: a guest who cannot find or redeem a token they paid for is a
  worse failure than an ugly panel.
- *Access and gating:* these controls are drawn for readers who may not be
  entitled to press them; every refusal path must stay reachable and readable.
- *Venue secrecy:* **none of these four reads a venue** — measured. Stated so it
  stays true, and asserted by grep rather than assumed.

**Why the conversion makes the guards tighter rather than looser.** None of the
four hand-rolled shells handles Escape, traps focus, or makes the document inert.
The platform gives all three through the modal dialog. And the house rule puts
Cancel first in the DOM, first in the tab order, with the initial-focus marker the
primitive honours **imperatively after the modal opens** — because the framework's
autofocus prop is skipped by the attribute writer and never reaches the DOM at
all. A confirmation that opens with Cancel focused and refuses Enter is measurably
harder to trigger by accident than one that opens with nothing focused.

**What this conversion cannot make better, and says so.** There is **no error
tracking** in this repository, so no production failure on this path reaches a
human by itself. Four silent catches on the guest money path are recorded in this
phase's `deferred-items.md` by plan 41.2-01 and are **not fixed here**: a visual
conversion is not where the product decides what a buyer is told when a purchase
fails.
