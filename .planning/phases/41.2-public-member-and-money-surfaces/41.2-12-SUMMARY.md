# Phase 41.2 Plan 12: The Bar's Guest Path — Summary

**The two files a person without an account pays through are on the primitives; every path
between that person's browser custody and the redeem control is byte-identical, proved by
its absence from the diff rather than asserted; and the four failures that can leave a paid
guest holding nothing are still written down at `file:line` instead of quietly patched.**

**One conversion the plan asked for was refused, and saying so is the substance of this
summary rather than a footnote.** The plan modelled `GuestTokenDisplay.tsx` as carrying one
hand-rolled shell. Measured, it holds **three**, and two of them are the screens a bartender
operates on the guest's phone. That is F-10-01 arriving on the other half of a declared
fork, and it was answered the same way — and **without inheriting D-41.2-06**, which is the
twin's exemption and says in terms that an exemption is granted per file.

## The two bartender screens: what was refused, and why

**This is the plan's largest deviation and it is deliberate.** Measured on the base commit,
`GuestTokenDisplay.tsx` carries three overlays, not one:

```
:158  fixed inset-0 z-[100] … bg-background/95 backdrop-blur-md   ← the SERVED screen
:182  fixed inset-0 z-[100] … bg-background/95 backdrop-blur-md   ← the ACTIVE screen
:222  fixed inset-0 z-[60]  … bg-black/60 backdrop-blur-sm        ← the guest's confirmation
```

The plan's `<read_first>` names *"the hand-rolled dialog shell at `:222-223`"*. There are
three, and only the third is the one the plan describes. The file itself declares why —
`:65-66` of the base commit: *"fork of RedeemConfirmationModal"*.

**The argument was made on this file's evidence, not inherited.** D-41.2-06 grants a
permanent exemption to the twin's two overlays and says in terms that an exemption is
granted per file, *"or it becomes a way to make a list empty."* So:

- the serve area *"takes the whole screen above the Cancel row"* — the file's own comment,
  unchanged through the diff — so a bartender does not have to aim, at a counter, in the
  dark, with a queue. Inside the primitive it becomes a panel body bounded by the shell's
  own maximum, and the act it performs (`serve`) is the **irreversible** one;
- the narrow Cancel row beneath it would become a full-width control in the `actions`
  region, directly under the thumb — the reverting act made **easier** while the
  irreversible one is made harder. That is the guard moving the wrong way, which is the
  exact inverse of what a money conversion is allowed to do;
- the SERVED screen is a full-bleed announcement read across a counter, dismissed by a tap
  anywhere and by a three-second timer. Inside a dialog it acquires a heading, a close
  control and the primitive's own close routes, which would then compete with the timer.
  That is behaviour, not markup.

**Their inks were converted; their geometry was not.** A token substitution moves nothing on
screen but the colour, so both screens now carry semantic ink — and the file reaches **0**
raw palette and **0** legacy tokens all the same.

**Consequences, stated rather than hidden:**

- `grep -c 'fixed inset-0'` and `grep -c 'backdrop-blur'` each return **2**, not 0.
- The dialog ratchet's entry for this file is correctly **not** STALE, and `REMAINING` stayed
  at **4** rather than falling to 3.
- **The overlays were left legible to the matcher rather than rewritten into a shape it
  cannot see.** Making a number go quiet without the thing changing is the fifth mechanism
  this phase recorded in wave 4, and it is not available as a shortcut here.
- **No exemption was granted by this plan.** A refusal is recorded and the debt stays
  declared; whether it becomes an exemption is the owner's, on this file's argument.
