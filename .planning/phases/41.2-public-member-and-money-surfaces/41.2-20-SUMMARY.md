## The union, and how it was assembled

**The 28 are not re-enumerated as 84 rows, and that is the load-bearing choice.** Their rows already exist: Phase 41's **O1** says *"the eight surfaces this phase declares, at 390px / 768px / 1280px, on a device"*, and Phase 41.1's **O8** says *"every surface this phase converted, at 390px / 768px / 1280px"* — 8 + 20 = 28. What replaced re-enumeration is **§2, a coverage map with no Result column**: all 28 routes named, each against the owed row that covers it, plus the extra rows that touch it (O2 on the four focus routes, O3 on `/admin/formats`, O4 and O9 on the tables, O11 on the event edit surface, O12 on the two refund surfaces, O13 on the guest-list surface). ## Deviations from Plan

### `[Rule 3 — reading resolved]` The plan's "each entry gets a `DI-41.2-NN`" against what 129 entries actually are

**Found during:** task 3, on reading all seventeen files.

**The issue.** The plan's acceptance criterion reads *"Each entry in each of those files has a corresponding `DI-41.2-NN` entry"*. Most of the 129 are **not deferred items**: they are measurements of record, decisions taken with their arguments written down, repairs that landed inside their own plan, human rows routed to the two passes, and corrections to figures in planning documents. `41.2-16-FINDINGS.md` alone is an eighteen-section knot impact analysis whose §6 states in its own words that **nothing on the money path is reachable from that file**.

**The reading applied.** The plan's own field list resolves it: it asks each entry to carry *"why it is recorded rather than fixed"* — a field that only exists for an entry that **is** deferred. So the 35 headings that carry that field became the 32 DI entries, and the other 94 are **roll-called with their headings verbatim, their file and their disposition** — where the disposition is taken from **each entry's own status words** (*"Recorded, not repaired"*, *"Applied, not deferred"*, *"a documentation drift, closed here"*), never invented.

**Why the roll-call rather than 129 DI numbers.** `deferred-items.md`'s own header says what it holds: *"Out-of-scope findings measured while executing this phase … not fixed here."* Giving a measurement a debt number puts things on a debt list that are **not debt** — which is the same error, in the opposite direction, as letting a debt go quiet. The criterion the plan was reaching for — **none silently dropped** — is met exactly: all 129 appear, and each appears as what it is.
