### The one that costs a member something — recorded, never repaired

`dashboard/page.tsx` performs four reads and **discards the error object on all four**
(`:173`, `:200`, `:214`, `:256`). On the token read at `:214` the consequence is exact: a
failed query leaves the array null, the grouping coalesces it to empty, and the *My Drinks*
section **is not drawn at all** — so a member who paid for drinks and whose read failed sees
a dashboard identical to that of a member who bought nothing. The ticket read at `:200` has
the same shape and renders *No tickets yet* beside an invitation to discover events.

It is worse than the ordinary form of this defect, and worth saying why: there is no error
tracking in this repository, so the failure reaches nobody by itself, and the wrong answer is
not a blank screen a person distrusts — it is a **confident, well-formatted, false statement
about what they own**. `meta-gates.md` asks for an observable effect and says "log it" is not
sufficient; this one does not even log.

**It was not fixed here.** Distinguishing *you have no tokens* from *we could not read your
tokens* is new copy on a money surface, it needs a sentence that does not manufacture alarm,
and it needs the same treatment on all four reads or it is an inconsistency rather than a
fix. That is exactly the reason the four recorded catches were recorded rather than repaired.
The four reads are unchanged context lines in the diff, in both directions.
