### 7.2 The non-obvious choices, each with its mechanical reason

**The lineup pills are `Chip`, and the accent goes with them.** `41-UI-SPEC.md`
§6.4 names **this file, by line**, as the tree's canonical 20 px target, and §8.5
gives the rule: *a badge that is a `<Link>` or a `<button>` is a Chip.* The pill

The accent tint is a **loss, not a correction** — §5.1 lists the lineup pills
among the four things the accent is for. Both roads that would have kept it were
refused with a reason, in `41.2-15-FINDINGS.md` §4: the chip's accent **fill** is
its `selected` state, which is also the only thing that emits `aria-current`, and
a pill that is not the current anything may not claim to be; and re-applying the
tint from the call site is the **WR-05 trap** — same property, same specificity,
so the emitted stylesheet's order decides rather than the class list. Closing it
needs a new chip rung on a **spine** file, and **41.2 does not edit the spine.**

**The internal links stayed links.** *View Your Event Pass*, *View Your Ticket*
and the drink-menu link kept `<Link>` / `<a>` and took the ladder's md-rung

the file: the button ladder's `href` branch renders a **bare anchor** typed
`string`, so an internal address loses client-side navigation, prefetching **and**
the build-time check that the route exists — silently, all three. **That is a
behaviour change dressed as a styling choice, and it was avoided in the choosing
rather than discovered afterwards** — the same trap 41.2-08 avoided on the wallet
control, in the opposite direction.
