this plan carries is not *did the layout survive*: it is **did anything become
visible to somebody who could not see it before, or become visible earlier.**

this same wave, on disjoint file sets — the same split the previous phase used for
its 1669-line form and its five satellites — and the surface is declared only
after all four land.

hard to trip as it was.
