## F-41.2-05-01 — Check E's pairing was ALREADY equal before this plan declared anything

**Severity:** informational, and it corrects a belief three later plans would otherwise
inherit.
**Disposition:** recorded. Nothing was changed on account of it.

The plan's own framing is that declaring the three surfaces is what brings them into
both halves of check E's pairing — *"three files should now appear in **both** the
declaring set and the mounting set"*. **They already did, before this plan's first
edit.** Measured on the base commit, with `CONVERTED` still holding 28:

```
          file(s) DECLARING the column clearance            : 5
          file(s) MOUNTING the responsive form              : 5
```

and the five are `(work)/layout.tsx`, `(public)/gallery/page.tsx`, `src/app/page.tsx`,
`(public)/newsletter/page.tsx` and `(public)/artists/[slug]/page.tsx` — the two baseline
files plus wave 1's three. After this plan's declarations the numbers are **the same
five over the same five**.

**Why, read from the source rather than inferred.** The pairing is computed from two
tree-wide reads and **not** from `CONVERTED`: the declaring side is a grep for the
inline-axis declaration over all 264 files under `src/`, and the mounting side is
`importsDirectly` over the same walk (`verify-conversion.mjs:2803-2809`). Neither
consults the manifest. So the pairing moved 2/2 → 5/5 **in wave 1's own commits**, and
this plan could not have moved it.

**What this plan's declarations DID move, so the two are not confused:**

| Figure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| surfaces declared converted | 28 | **31** |
| surfaces whose width was compared against §4 | 28 | **31** |
| surfaces mounting a navigation, in the route table | 24 of 28 | **27 of 31** |
| the pairing, both sides | 5 / 5 | 5 / 5 — **unmoved** |

**Why it matters to a later plan and not only to this one.** Seven more surfaces convert
in this phase, and each of their reconciliations will be told the same thing. A
reconciliation that reads its own pairing figure as *evidence that its declaration
landed* is reading a number its declaration cannot touch — which is the debt-goes-quiet
shape one step removed: not a counter falling for the wrong reason, but a counter
**credited** to the wrong cause. **The figure that a declaration actually moves is the
route-table partition and the width-against-§4 comparison**, and those are what a
reconciliation should quote.

## F-41.2-05-04 — A whole-file substitution edited two entries this plan does not own

**Severity:** caught before the commit; the committed diff is 57 insertions and **zero**
deletions to `CONVERTED`.
**Disposition:** reverted in the working tree, then recorded — because the near-miss is
the interesting part, not the outcome.

The acceptance criterion asks that `LC_ALL=C /usr/bin/grep -c 'no query changed'` on the
manifest rise by at least 3. The three new entries had been written with the
no-behaviour-change sentence capitalised at the start of a sentence, so the count did not
move at all (13 → 13). The repair was a `sed` over the file for the sentence, and **it
matched two pre-existing entries as well** — `/admin/artists` and `/admin/newsletter`,
both plan 41.1-06's — rewriting reason text this plan has no authority over.

`git diff` showed three `-` lines where there should have been none. The two were
restored by a second `sed` restricted to lines naming that plan, and the diff went to
pure insertion before anything was staged.

**Why it is written down rather than quietly fixed.** The block this plan appends to
opens by refusing exactly this: *"an editorial fix to somebody else's sentence is exactly
the move this block's opening paragraph refuses"*, and the wave-3 block says the same at
length — *"the plan that walked the closure is the one that knows what whole turned out
to mean, and a reconciliation re-writing those sentences would be asserting about files
it did not open."* A whole-file substitution is how that rule gets broken **by accident**
rather than by decision, and the defence is the same one this wave exists to apply: read
the diff, not the count.

**Final state, asserted:** `grep -c 'no query changed'` **13 → 16**, and
`git diff --stat` on the manifest reports **57 insertions, 0 deletions**.
