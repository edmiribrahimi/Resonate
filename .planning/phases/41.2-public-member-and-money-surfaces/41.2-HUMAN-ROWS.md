## How to measure, so the result is a number

**The leading column clearance.** From 768px up the responsive navigation is a
**side column**, not a bottom bar, and the shell reserves **224px** on the leading
edge for it. Read the distance from the viewport's leading edge to the leftmost
pixel of any content. It must be **224px, within 8px**. Below 768px that same
distance must be **0px, within 4px** — a column reserved on a phone is the silent
half of the defect check E pairs against.

**Content centring inside the reserved area.** The shell centres a **1024px**
content column inside whatever is left after the clearance, with **24px** of its
own gutter each side. So read the space between the navigation column's trailing
edge and the content's leading edge, and the space between the content's trailing
edge and the viewport's trailing edge: **the two must differ by no more than
8px**, which puts the column's centre within 4px of the centre of the space it
lives in.

**Horizontal scroll.** Put a finger, or the trackpad, on the page body and drag
sideways. The page must move **0px**. Not *barely*, not *a little*: zero.

**The bottom clearance on a phone.** The shell reserves the navigation bar's own
height plus **16px**. Scroll to the end of the page: the last element's bottom
edge must clear the bar's top edge by **at least 16px**, within 4px.

**A control's height.** Read the box, not the ink. **44px is a MINIMUM, not a
fixed height** — a control whose label wraps to two lines is taller and is
correct; a control at 43px is not.

**Reaching the three surfaces.** `/` and `/newsletter` are public and need no
session. `/artists/[slug]` is public too, but its edit affordance is drawn only
for an account holding the organizer or master role — the dialog rows need such a
session, and **no row here creates, edits or deletes anything**: the dialog is
opened, looked at, and dismissed with Escape.
