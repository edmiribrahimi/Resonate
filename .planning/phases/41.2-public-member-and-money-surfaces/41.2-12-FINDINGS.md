## F-41.2-12-01 — `GuestTokenDisplay.tsx` carries THREE overlays, not one, and two of them were refused

**Severity:** it changes two of the plan's acceptance criteria and leaves an entry on a
ratchet list that the plan predicted would go STALE.
**Disposition:** the guest's confirmation converted; the other two refused, their **inks**
converted, their **geometry** untouched, and both left legible to the dialog gate.

`41.2-12-PLAN.md` names *"the hand-rolled dialog shell at `:222-223`"* — one shell — and
its acceptance criterion asks for `fixed inset-0`, `backdrop-blur` and the stacking
literal to each return **0**. Measured on the base commit, the file carries **three**
overlays:

```
:158  fixed inset-0 z-[100] flex items-center justify-center bg-background/95 backdrop-blur-md   ← the SERVED screen
:182  fixed inset-0 z-[100] flex flex-col bg-background/95 backdrop-blur-md                      ← the ACTIVE screen
:222  fixed inset-0 z-[60]  flex items-end sm:items-center justify-center bg-black/60 …          ← the guest's confirmation
```

Only `:222` is the shell the plan describes. **This is F-10-01 arriving on the other half
of the fork**, which the file itself declares at `:65-68` of the base commit: *"fork of
RedeemConfirmationModal"*.

### Why the other two were refused, argued on THIS file's evidence

The argument is not inherited. **D-41.2-06 grants a permanent exemption to
`RedeemConfirmationModal.tsx`'s two overlays and says in terms that an exemption is
granted per file, on that file's argument, "or it becomes a way to make a list empty."**
So this file states its own, and the evidence is the file's own comment, which survives
the diff unchanged:

```
:183 (base) / :283 (now)  {/* Big tap-to-serve area: takes the whole screen above the Cancel row */}
```

- The **serve** screen is operated by a **bartender, on the guest's phone**. Its whole
  affordance is that the tap target is the screen — the bartender does not have to aim,
  at a counter, in the dark, with a queue. Inside the primitive it becomes a panel body
  bounded by the shell's own maximum width and height: the bartender has to aim, and the
  act it performs (`serve`) is the **irreversible** one — `active → redeemed`, the drink
  poured. Making the irreversible act harder to hit and the reverting act easier is the
  guard moving the wrong way.
- The **Cancel** row beneath it is deliberately small — the same 28px-class target the
  twin carries with its reason written down. Inside the primitive it becomes a control in
  the `actions` region: full width, at the bottom of the panel, directly under the thumb.
- The **SERVED** screen is a full-bleed announcement that both the bartender and the guest
  read from a distance, dismissed by a tap anywhere and by a 3-second timer. Inside a
  dialog it acquires a heading, a close control and the primitive's own close routes,
  which would then compete with the timer. That is behaviour, not markup.

**Not gamed.** Both overlays were left in the shape the gate's matcher can see, rather
than rewritten into one it cannot. A number made quiet without the thing changing is the
fifth mechanism this phase recorded in wave 4 (`41.2-09-FINDINGS.md` F-41.2-09-02).

### The measured consequence

| Criterion | Plan asks | Measured after |
|---|---|---|
| `fixed inset-0` | 0 | **2** |
| `backdrop-blur` | 0 | **2** |
| the modal stacking literal `z-[60]` | 0 | **0** ✓ |
| `verify-dialogs` reports this entry **STALE** | yes | **no** — the entry is correctly still live |

`REMAINING` therefore stays at **4** rather than falling to 3, and `verify-dialogs` exits
**0** with `DIALOGS_OK`. The breakpoints entry *did* go STALE, and the pair disagreeing is
the two lists each telling the truth about a different thing — exactly as it does for the
twin.

**Route:** a plan that owns the bar's operating surface, with a person behind the counter
— the same route F-10-01 took, and the same argument the owner accepted there. **This
plan grants no exemption**; it records a refusal and leaves the debt declared.

## F-41.2-12-05 — one user-visible word changed, and it is the word the accessible name already carried

**Severity:** it is the single copy delta in 381 changed lines (274 inserted, 107 deleted),
and it is stated rather than buried.
**Disposition:** taken, with the precedent named.

The completed-token card marked *redeemed* with a green tick glyph carrying
`aria-label="Redeemed"`. The tick is now the neutral badge, and its text is that same
word:

```
-          <span className="shrink-0 text-green-400 text-lg" aria-label="Redeemed">
-            &#10003;
+          <Badge className="shrink-0">Redeemed</Badge>
```

Three reasons it was taken rather than preserved:

1. **Nothing a screen reader hears changed** — the word was already the element's
   accessible name. A sighted reader now reads what a screen reader was already given.
2. **The raw hue had to go regardless** (the palette grep must reach 0), and D-41.1-25
   refuses a tone per outcome — so the alternative was a colourless tick, which says less
   than the word.
3. **The other half of this fork made the identical move one wave earlier**, on the
   identical component. Two forks of one card disagreeing about how a redeemed drink
   reads is the "two implementations disagree" defect this phase exists to remove.

Everything else is byte-identical, proved by an extractor that **refuses unless it first
reproduces a known difference on a reworded fixture and a known silence on an identical
one**:

```
  self-check  mutation actually applied : yes
  self-check  reworded input -> differs  : yes (expected)
  self-check  identical input -> equal   : yes (expected)
  self-check PASSED
```

`aria-label="Close"` also leaves this file — and is **re-supplied identically by the
primitive** at `Dialog.tsx:290`, so no accessible name moved.
