> ## ⚠ D-41.2-06 — two shells are a DECLARED EXEMPTION, and this plan implements it *(owner, 2026-08-14)*
>
> `RedeemConfirmationModal.tsx`'s **two bartender overlays** do not convert and never will.

> Money going back, at a counter, at two in the morning, with the guard loosened.
>
> **This plan moves those two from `REMAINING` to the gate's DECLARED EXEMPTION list, with
> the reason travelling in the gate itself — not in a summary.** The shape is the one
> `Lightbox.tsx` already has, and its rule is the argument: *a file that will never convert is
> not a debt.*
>
> **Criterion 3 then closes honestly rather than by relaxation:** the debt list is empty, and
> what remains is *declared correct* instead of *forgotten*. The gate already keeps the two
> facts apart — **exempt** means a person measured the file and declared it correct, **fenced**
> means nobody measured it at all. **An exemption without its reason is indistinguishable from
> a file nobody got to.** Write the reason with the entry.
>
> **Do not extend this to any other file by analogy.** An exemption is granted per file, on
> that file's own argument, or it becomes a technique for making a list empty. If plan
> 41.2-14 declares one for `MyMediaSection.tsx`, it carries its own reason or it does not ship.

> ## ⚠ A FIFTH mechanism for "the debt goes quiet": a corroborating instrument that measures nothing
>
> **Found in wave 4, and it nearly authorised twelve wrong deletions.** The reconciliation's
> second instrument — the one whose whole job is to corroborate the gate without going
> through the shared comment stripper — returned **`0/0/0` on all eight files at its first
> run**, including four the gates report with 3, 1, 1 and 3 overlays. The defect was in the
> instrument: a character class defined *with* brackets and then used *inside* brackets. It
> was caught only because a plain `grep -c 'fixed'` on the same file returned 3.
>
> **Had it been believed**, the operating rule — *delete an entry when the gate and a second
> instrument both read zero* — would have authorised deleting **every** entry, in a wave
> whose expected outcome was zero deletions, and **each deletion would have carried a second
> instrument's agreement.** That is a fifth mechanism alongside the four already recorded:
> not a fence, not an unmeasured Map, not a walk-membership gap, not a blind lexer — an
> instrument that measures nothing and therefore agrees with everything.
>
> **The rule this yields, and it binds every zero in this plan:**
>
> **A zero counts only if the run that produced it printed its own self-check.** Any
> corroborating instrument must first reproduce a **known non-zero on a positive fixture**
> and a **known zero on a negative one**, and **refuse with exit 2** if it cannot. An
> instrument that has not proved it can see is not evidence of absence — it is silence
> wearing the costume of a measurement.

closure by a mutation that is asserted applied before its result is read.

Purpose: **this is criterion 3, and it is the criterion most easily faked.** A
`REMAINING` list at zero is a *claim*, not evidence, and this project has watched
the debt-goes-quiet defect return through **four distinct mechanisms** — a
never-measured map, a fence match, an unguarded walk-membership check, and a
comment stripper going blind inside a file. Worse, a substitution that **silently
failed to match** has already produced a false negative here: a mutation nobody
applied proves nothing and looks exactly like a mutation that was applied and
caught.

So each closure is proved twice over: the list is emptied only on entries
re-derived from the tree with a second instrument the gates do not share, and the
gate is then shown to still have teeth by a mutation whose **application is
asserted before its result is read.**

Both gates **refuse an empty list on purpose** — exit 2, nothing measured — until
a written closure decision sits above the constant and the guard reads it. That
refusal is not an obstacle to route around: it is the mechanism that makes an
emptiness a decision rather than a list that quietly emptied itself while nobody
was reading. One gate in this tree has already made the transition, and its shape
is copied rather than invented.

Output: thirty-eight declared surfaces, two closed migrations, two proved-red
mutations, and check F settled the way wave 0 said.
</objective>
