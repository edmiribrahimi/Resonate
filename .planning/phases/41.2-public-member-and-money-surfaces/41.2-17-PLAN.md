<objective>
Convert the money satellites of the public event page: the ticket tier selection,
the member's drinks list, and the handler that resumes a purchase after a return
from the provider.

Purpose: these three sit on the same file set boundary as knot 2 and run in the
same wave, on **disjoint files**. The tier selection is the surface that decides
**what a guest pays** — its inputs carry a price, a quantity, a discount value and
a usage ceiling, and each of those carries a type, a required flag, a minimum and
a step. The previous phase established the instrument for proving those unchanged:
a **multiset over comment-stripped source**, rather than reading a diff, because a
diff read by eye is where a step or a minimum goes missing.

The pending-intent handler is the quietest of the three and the least forgiving: it
decides whether a purchase a person already started is picked back up when they
return. A condition changed there is a purchase that silently does not resume, on
a page with no error tracking behind it.

Output: three files, one validation multiset proved equal, and a purchase path
that resumes on the same conditions it did.
</objective>
