narrowed by the writing around it.** A hint made precise by its caption is a
reveal with extra steps. A conversion adds no caption.

is a raw control with no declared minimum today, and the two links inside are
below the touch floor. On the primitives they reach it. **Nothing about who may
open this dialog changes; only how easily a finger hits it.**

## Written before the diff — required by the Response Gate and by the knot rule

**Classification: Critical.** This component sits on the boundary between *a
person is told where the night is* and *a person is told what they would have to
do to be told.* `venue_reveal_sent` is **monotone**: once an address is out it is
out, and a screenshot is not recalled.

**Domain:** `venue-secrecy` primary; `access-gating` and `community-membership`
secondary; `meta-gates.md`'s monotone guard binds.

**What is opened: exactly one file** —
`src/app/(public)/events/[slug]/SecretVenueDialog.tsx`, 176 lines.

**What is not opened, and must not be:** the page that mounts it, which is plan
41.2-15's and runs **in this same wave on a disjoint file set**; the two other
satellite plans of this wave; the visibility function, which lives in the page;
the per-night public venue view, which is a database object; and the reveal path
itself, whose dialog and panel were converted a phase earlier.

**Cross-domain impact:**

- *Venue secrecy:* this is the negative branch. Everything it renders is chosen so
  as **not** to be the address. A conversion may restyle it and may not narrow it.
- *Access and gating:* the three branches correspond to three states of a reader —
  not signed in, signed in and not approved, approved. A merged branch is a reader
  shown a path that is not theirs, or shown one that is.
- *Community and membership:* the two bullets describe the two ways in. **They are
  not the same way**, and the file says so in place. Making them read the same is
  a product statement about how somebody gets into a night.

**Why the conversion makes the guard tighter rather than looser, and where it does
not.** Tighter: the hand-rolled overlay handles no Escape, traps no focus and
leaves the document behind it live; the platform's modal supplies all three. The
trigger and the two inner links reach the touch floor. **Not looser, and this is
the sentence that must be earned**: nothing here shortens the path between opening
this dialog and being told an address, because this dialog never tells one.

**Focus placement, and it is a decision to state rather than inherit.** The
primitive focuses the element carrying the initial-focus marker if the caller
declares one, and otherwise the close control — *which is first in the DOM and is
the least destructive control by construction.* **This dialog has no destructive
control**: its actions are two ways in and a way out. So the marker either goes on
the way out, or is omitted and the default does the same thing. **Say which**,
because the previous phase's own correction is what happens when two mechanisms
sit there for one intent.

**One withdrawn claim, so it is not cited.** A previous pattern map argued that a
framework autofocus attribute in the reveal dialog was inert and that the focus
placement was therefore unspecified in practice. **The converted file corrected
that with its own measurement**: the imperative call one screen above it landed,
and the control *was* focused — it simply was not visible. **The live argument is
about the attribute, in the primitive, and not about any file.** A plan citing the
withdrawn claim would be citing a claim this tree has already retracted.
