## Deviations from Plan

**None on the plan's instructions.** Three judgements the plan left to execution,
each recorded with its reason:

**1. The ticket card is a plain container, not the card primitive.**
The cover image bleeds to the card's edge, so the card carries no padding of its
own. The primitive declares one, and passing a padding override would make the
result depend on Tailwind's emission order — the trap WR-05 already recorded in
this tree. The converted tree already writes this shape out at
`src/components/media/MediaReviewGrid.tsx:164` and
`src/app/(admin)/admin/newsletter/BroadcastList.tsx:164`, both for the same
reason. The tokens are the converted ones, so both clean-file greps return zero.

**2. The wallet control took a ladder rung.** The four-variant ladder has no
inverted-fill rung and adding one would edit `ui/Button.tsx` — **spine, a halt**.
So the choice was a rung or a hand-written off-ladder fill, and the rung was
taken. The hue changes; the element, the address and the absence of prefetch do
not. Declared as a human row rather than shipped quietly.

**3. The back link kept its shape and gained the floor.** It is a text link, not
a pill, so it took the converted text-link shape already in the tree
(`(work)/events/[id]/drinks/page.tsx:123-128`) — an unprefixed 44px minimum, the
imported focus expression, and the converted hover ink — rather than becoming a
control it is not.

**Nothing was repaired that this plan found and does not own.** The refusal in
`RefundRequestButton.tsx` that collapses three distinct causes into one sentence
was recorded by wave 0's plan 41.2-02 and is **not** repaired here; the file was
not opened.
