<objective>
Write the two mechanical checks this phase needs and does not have: the redirect-table walk,
and the `revalidatePath` check.

Purpose: STAFF-02 has no automated instrument otherwise — the redirect table is fifteen rows of
prose until something requests all fifteen. And `revalidatePath` is the phase's **silent-failure
category**: 36 calls, untyped in every option, invisible to `typedRoutes` (whose augmentation
covers `next/link`, `next/navigation` and `next/form`, never `next/cache`) and invisible to
`npm run build`. Its failure mode is a members list that stops refreshing after an approval —
a member-facing consequence of a routing edit, in a product with **no error tracking** to
report it (D-34-16).

A shell loop is re-runnable; a prose procedure is not. Both of these are committed scripts for
that reason.

Output: `scripts/verify-organizer-redirects.sh`, `scripts/verify-routes.mjs`, two `package.json`
entries, and two mutation proofs.
</objective>
