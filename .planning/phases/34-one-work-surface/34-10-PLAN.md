<phase_rule>
**R-WORK-ROUTES — only Next.js route files enter `(work)`.** Declared in plan 34-07, Task 1,
and cited here rather than re-derived. `page.tsx` and `loading.tsx` move into
`src/app/(admin)/admin/(work)/…`; `actions.ts` and co-located client components **stay** at
their `src/app/(admin)/admin/…` path, outside the group, exactly where plan 34-07 put them.
A moved page reaches its former siblings with an absolute `@/app/(admin)/admin/…` specifier —
an edit inside the page, owned by this plan and by no other.

The rule is what keeps this wave parallel. A route group governs routing and nothing else, so
a non-route module gains nothing by entering `(work)`, while moving it changes its specifier
for **every external importer** — and those importers are files owned by sibling plans in this
same wave. In parallel worktrees a sibling's `git mv` is invisible to `grep`, so the outcome
is either a merge conflict or a clean merge pointing at a file that no longer exists.
</phase_rule>
