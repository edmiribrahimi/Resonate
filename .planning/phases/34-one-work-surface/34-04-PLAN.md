<objective>
Make the two staff menus read the same declaration the middleware reads, so a drawn entry
always has a matching server-side check. Delete `StaffNav`'s `context` prop and
`ManagementSection`'s two hand-maintained href lists.

Purpose: STAFF-03. Today there are **three** hand-maintained menus, not the two
`34-CONTEXT.md` counted — `ManagementSection` is the third, with two literal lists of seven
and four hrefs, and it is the one most likely to be forgotten. Two of the three filter on
role; one filters on role *and* status. None of them can be right for a role that did not
exist when they were written.

Output: two capability-driven staff menus, and a bottom nav whose one staff entry stops being
a string literal.

**Scope fence taken deliberately, and stated so it is reviewable.** `MobileNav`'s props are
**not** changed. It is mounted on **44 pages**, including every `(public)` and `(members)`
page and `src/app/(admin)/admin/scanner/page.tsx` — and the scanner file must not be modified
by this phase. Its three public entries (`/`, `/events`, `/gallery`) are not
capability-gated and are named out of scope in `34-CONTEXT.md`'s Deferred Ideas. Its fourth
entry is the **door**, which is Phase 39's. So `getVisibleNavItems(role, status)` keeps its
signature and its role filter, and gains only this: the Check-in href comes from the map
instead of a literal. The residual divergence — a `pending` organizer is shown no Check-in tab
while `door.operate` (`requires_approved = false`) would admit them — is **recorded as a
finding for Phase 39**, not silently closed here, because closing it means editing the door's
page.
</objective>
