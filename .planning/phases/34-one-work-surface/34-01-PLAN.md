binding each of the twelve capability keys either to the routes it opens or, explicitly, to

(`33-CONTEXT.md`). Every later plan in Phase 34 *reads* these two modules; none extends them.
That is what buys the fan-out in Waves 2 and 3.

**The direction of the collapse is decided and is not this plan's to revisit.** D-34-01:
`/admin` is the canonical prefix and `/organizer/*` redirects into it. The two prefixes are
symmetric in cost — 48 hardcoded references against 51 — so the tie is broken by the door.

would turn `/admin` into a redirect shell that has to **exclude** `/admin/scanner`, recreating
in a second place exactly the precedence hazard `middleware.ts:345-352` exists to warn about.
The collapse must not add a second place where the door's precedence has to be remembered.
Every row of the redirect table therefore points **from** `/organizer` **to** `/admin`, one
direction only, and never the reverse.

Output: `src/lib/routes/capability-routes.ts`, `src/lib/routes/organizer-redirects.ts`,
`typedRoutes: true`, and two mutation proofs recorded in the SUMMARY.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → middleware | `pathname` is attacker-controlled and reaches `resolveRoute` and `resolveOrganizerRedirect` |
| build → runtime | The map is the declaration three readers share; a wrong entry is a wrong verdict everywhere at once |
