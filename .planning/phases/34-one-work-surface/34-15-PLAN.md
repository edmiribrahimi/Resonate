*"Quattro route group, e il gruppo **è** il pubblico"*. After D-34-02 the group is **not** the
audience, and that is accepted deliberately. A gate that tells a future reader that putting a
file in `(admin)` is an access decision, when it no longer is, is the exact failure
`meta-gates.md` records as **worse than no table**.
