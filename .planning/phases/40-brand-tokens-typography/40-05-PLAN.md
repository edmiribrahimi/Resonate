<objective>
**Classification: Critical. This plan touches the door.**

DS-10 says no device is left serving a mixture of old and new styles. Research
replaced the assumed failure with the measured one, and the plan must carry that
correction rather than rediscover it.

**What is already correct, so nobody builds a mechanism for it.** Next 16.1.6
stamps every RSC response with the build ID: on a navigation the router refuses to
stitch two generations and serves the URL the person tapped as one whole document
(`fetch-server-response.js:142-144`); on a prefetch it **rejects the entry and does
not navigate** (four call sites in `segment-cache/cache.js`). The "half-applied
page from a link" cannot be produced. Zero configuration, already active.

**What is actually broken, with its two causing lines named.**
`Serwist.handleActivate` deletes from the precache every entry absent from the new
manifest (`serwist/dist/index.js:1228-1245`), and CSS filenames are content hashes
— so the previous stylesheet is deleted the moment the new worker activates, while
the old page is still open (`skipWaiting: true`). Meanwhile documents reach the
`pages` bucket by two paths, and one of them —`cacheOnNavigation: true` at
`next.config.ts:7` — writes straight into `caches.open("pages")`, bypassing every
runtime route, and **never refreshes what it wrote** (`if (isPageCached) return;`).

> Night N−1: `/door` is opened online on the staff phone; its document enters
> `pages`, its `<link>` naming a stylesheet. A release ships. Night N, within 24 h,
> radio off: the cached document is served and the stylesheet it names is no longer
> on the device and cannot be fetched. **An unstyled door, in a dark room, in front
> of a queue.**

Purpose: close that hole structurally (D-40-13), and write the only proof DS-10
will ever have before the release it tests.
Output: an additional `activate` listener, `cacheOnNavigation: false`, and
`40-RELEASE-PASS.md`.

**The accepted cost, stated plainly rather than minimised.** After a release, the
first open of any page on a device must be online. For `/door` that is the runbook
line `checkin-offline.md:57` already requires — it adds no step; it makes an
existing step load-bearing in one more situation. It is the same trade the owner
decided on 2026-08-11 (`checkin-offline.md:59`), in the direction it was decided:
*«una porta servita da una cache di ieri e' una porta stantia»*. D-40-13 settles
it; this plan executes it and does not re-open it.
</objective>

`serwist.addEventListeners()`. It is an **additional** listener, never a

Inside it, wrap in `event.waitUntil` a `Promise.all` that calls `caches.delete` on
exactly the three document bucket names — `pages`, `pages-rsc`,
`pages-rsc-prefetch`. Nothing else. Do not touch the precache cache, do not iterate
`caches.keys()`, do not add a `controllerchange` handler, do not call
`clients.claim()` again, and **do not navigate or reload anything**: D-40-11 is
absolute and this mechanism satisfies it by construction, which is the reason it
was chosen over every alternative.

An `activate` event fires only when the worker's bytes changed — that is, only on a
release. So the release boundary is already an event and there is **no version
bookkeeping to invent**: no constant compared in `localStorage`, no `/version.json`
poll, no notice component. `40-RESEARCH.md` §13 lists each of those as a thing not
to hand-roll.

middle one is not optional:

  precache every entry absent from the new manifest

  previous stylesheet is gone the moment this worker activates. A document kept
  past that point names a stylesheet that is no longer on the device and cannot be
  fetched: offline, that is an unstyled page, and the page this matters for is

  `handleActivate` already own that, and they are different mechanisms

  `/events/*` `NetworkOnly` rule any weaker — it only ever **removes** copies,
  which is the direction `venue-secrecy.md` wants.
