<objective>
Stop the most public money surface in the product from printing counts it could not read.

Purpose: three reads on this page ask how many tickets a tier has sold and how many people
have RSVP'd. None destructures its error, and all three coalesce a failure to zero. A full
night therefore renders as open, with a remaining-places figure that is not a measurement,
beside the control that takes money.

**The mechanism is constrained twice, and both constraints are decisions.**

The owner's standing decision on this finding is that **the purchase control stays live and
the server refuses** — recorded at the head of `46-FINDING-01.md` and again in
`46-VALIDATION.md`'s manual row, which instructs the verifier to *"verify the refusal is
reachable and legible, not that the control disappears"*. So this plan does **not** remove
the control. What it removes is the false number. (`46-PATTERNS.md` §3.4 recommends the
opposite — not rendering the control — and it was written from the research's
recommendation, before O5 was answered. The owner's answer governs. This divergence is
flagged rather than resolved in silence.)

And **this file is the one where a wrong edit publishes an address**, and a publication has
no remedy. Four properties bind every diff.

Output: three destructured reads, a third state that reaches both call sites, one approved
sentence, and the four reveal derivations re-run and shown.
</objective>
