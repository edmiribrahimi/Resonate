<objective>
Give the organizer's menu-closing command an outcome, and give it **two** of them.

Purpose: `menu_closes_at` decides when a drink token can still be bought and, through the
one-hour grace period, when one can still be redeemed. Today the action throws on a refused
capability and on a failed write, neither `handleSave` nor `handleClear` catches, the
transition rejects, `setSaved(true)` is never reached, and **nothing at all appears**. An
organizer who believes they closed the menu at midnight and did not is a bar still selling
tokens at two.

And the cheap repair is the trap. D-46-10b: *you may not do this* and *it did not save* are
different facts to an organizer, and collapsing them into one catch would replace a silent
failure with a collapsed one — the newsletter defect, reproduced on a staff money surface.

Output: three categories carried as values, three sentences from the approved list, and a
region that draws them beside the confirmation the control already has.
</objective>
