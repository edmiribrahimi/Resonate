<objective>
Capture the permission matrix as it stands **before phase 36 exists on disk**, on
both targets, and commit it.

Purpose: `scripts/rls-baseline.mjs:6-11` states the rule this plan exists to keep —
*a baseline taken after the change is not a baseline*. Phase 36 adds two tables,
three `NOT NULL` columns, four policies and two constraints to the table that
carries the public gate. Without a `pre-36` point, `baseline:compare` at the end
of the phase has nothing to compare against and every cell that moved becomes
unexplainable rather than explained.

Output: six artefact files under
`.planning/phases/32-capability-model-in-the-database/baseline/`, all carrying
`phase_point: pre-36`.

**This plan must complete before `supabase/migrations/*_formats_and_series.sql`
exists.** Plan 36-03 depends on it for exactly that reason.
</objective>
