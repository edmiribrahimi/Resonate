    Create `src/lib/production/ics/classify.ts`.

    Export `classifyEntry(event, aliases): ClassifiedEntry`, where `aliases` is a
    `ReadonlyMap<string, string>` from a lower-cased venue word to a series code, supplied
    by the caller from `party_series.ics_alias`. THE ALIAS MAP IS AN ARGUMENT AND NEVER A
    LITERAL IN THIS FILE: a venue word for a space not yet acquired cannot be written into
    a public repository (D-44-04, `venue-acquisition.md`). Write that sentence in the
    docblock, and write the negative form of it too — this module contains no venue word,
    not even in an example.

    THREE GRAMMARS, not two. D-44-21 records two; the measurement found three, and a
    two-grammar parser silently drops three real pieces, two of which are the file's only
    genuine anchor overrides. Recognise, in this order:
      - CANONICAL PIECE: `<Kind> - <SIGLA>-<NNN>` with an optional third segment carrying
        the per-dj part marker. Kind vocabulary is exactly the six of `PIECE_KIND_LABELS`,
        matched case-insensitively against the label and never against a free string.
      - LEGACY INVERTED PIECE: `<FormatName> <NNN> - <Kind>`. Same kind vocabulary, no
        sigla, the number attached to the format word. Tag it `naming_convention: 'legacy'`.
      - NIGHT: `<FormatName>[ x <VenueWord>] <NNN>` — no kind token and NO SIGLA. This is
        the core of the join problem, and it is measurable: a matcher keyed on sigle finds
        ZERO nights.
    Everything else is class `unclassified` and carries its `uid` plus a reason code.

    THE INCLUSION RULE IS DECLARED, NOT IMPLIED (D-44-20). Export it as a documented
    constant listing, in prose the executor writes into the module: what enters, as what
    kind, and what is skipped. An entry with no recognised format word and no kind token is
    a `commitment` — it occupies a day and nothing more (D-44-18). An entry carrying a
    format word but no recognisable kind and no progressivo is `unclassified` and is
    RECORDED AND COUNTED, never guessed and never dropped: guessing hands it a format and a
    progressivo it does not have, which is the precise harm D-44-18 names.

    THE JOIN KEY. Export `joinKey(seriesCode, number)`. A night's key comes from resolving
    its venue word through the alias map to a series code; a canonical piece's key comes
    from its sigla; a legacy piece's key comes from its format word through the same map.
    Normalising all three grammars to ONE key is the whole point. Do NOT key on format plus
    number without the venue word: two satellite nights legitimately share the progressivo
    `001` under different series, and a join that ignores the venue word placed one
    satellite's listing at a POSITIVE offset from its night. Where a venue word has no
    alias, the entry is `unclassified` with the reason `alias_unresolved` — it is not
    guessed onto the nearest series.

    THE DURATION SIGNAL IS A WARNING AND NEVER A CLASSIFIER. Every piece in the file is a
    30-minute block in the late morning and nights are 4-, 7- or 8-hour evening blocks, but
    the day the owner books a piece at a different hour, a duration-based classifier
    silently reclassifies it. Export `durationDisagreement(event, entryClass): boolean` and
    report the disagreement as its own finding; never let it decide.

    FINDINGS STAY SEPARATE. `ClassificationResult` carries `nights`, `pieces`,
    `commitments`, `unclassified` and — as distinct lists —
    `aliasUnresolved`, `durationDisagreements`. Three findings are never one "problem"
    (`meta-gates.md`, the newsletter precedent). Every finding entry carries a `uid` and a
    reason code and NEVER a `SUMMARY`, a date or a venue word.

  <action>
    Create `src/lib/production/ics/anchors.ts`.

    Export the civil-date primitives this repository must own because no library may be
    trusted with them: `isoWeekday(date: CivilDate): 1..7` (Monday = 1), computed from the
    date parts by Zeller-style arithmetic and NOT by constructing a `Date`;
    `addDays(date: CivilDate, n: number): CivilDate` on a fixed days-in-month table with
    proleptic Gregorian leap years; `isoWeekStart(date: CivilDate): CivilDate`;
    `nearestWeekday(from: CivilDate, weekday: number, direction: 'before' | 'after' | 'on'):
    CivilDate`. Write the reason in the docblock: a conversion that moves a 22:00 entry
    across midnight moves its weekday, and a moved weekday turns a conforming night into a
    reported error.

    Export `resolveAnchor(rule, context): AnchorResolution` where `rule` carries
    `(anchor_kind, anchor_weekday, anchor_direction, derivable, episodes_from_lineup)` —
    read from `production_pipeline_rule`, passed in as a plain object, never hard-coded here.
    `context` carries the night's own civil date, the next edition's date when it exists,
    the next edition's listing date when it exists, and the count of credited artists when
    it is knowable.

    THE THREE ANCHOR KINDS, resolved as weekdays and never as counts:
      - `self` — resolve `anchor_weekday` relative to the night's own date in
        `anchor_direction`. Where `anchor_direction` is `on`, the answer is the night's own
        day.
      - `next_edition` — resolve inside the ISO WEEK CONTAINING THE NEXT NIGHT, which is
        where a night's LiveCuts fall in 6 of 6 measured editions. From its own night those
        same pieces look wildly irregular — spreads of 9 to 143 days — and that variability
        is in the point of observation, not in the data.
      - `next_edition_listing` — resolve relative to the next edition's LISTING, not to the
        night. Measured from its own night the after movie looked irregular; from the true
        anchor it is the day before, always.

    THE THREE REFUSALS STAY THREE, each with its own returned reason and never one
    collapsed "unknown" (`meta-gates.md`, the newsletter precedent):
      - `awaiting_next_edition` when the anchor edition is not in the calendar. The caller
        names the edition (D-44-12), and it covers LIVECUTS AS WELL AS THE AFTER MOVIE. A
        night in the archive with a listing and a timetable but no LiveCut and no after
        movie is THE RULE BEHAVING CORRECTLY and must never be "fixed".
      - `depends_on_lineup` when `episodes_from_lineup` is true and the credited-artist
        count is not knowable. Return no figure. Three is not a constant — one archived
        edition carries two.
      - `not_derivable` when `derivable` is false. This is the night's listing and SunSet's
        listing: both fall on a Tuesday, one to two and a half weeks out, with no fixed
        anticipation. D-44-23 withholds proposals for SunSet's; the measurement says the
        same withholding applies to the night's. NOTHING may compute either.

    Export `proposePieceDate(rule, context): { date: CivilDate } | { unresolved:
    UnresolvedReason; edition?: string }` as the single entry point the reconciler calls.
    A proposal produced here always carries `origin: 'proposed'` at the call site
    (D-44-09b part 3), and A DATE WRITTEN IN THE FILE ALWAYS WINS — this module is never
    consulted for a piece that exists in the file (D-44-09b part 1).

    Export `conformsToRule(actualDate, rule, context): boolean | null`, returning `null`
    when the rule cannot be resolved. This value feeds the divergence report of D-44-07 and
    REACHES NO PIXEL (D-44-10). State that in the docblock so the next reader does not
    render it.
  </action>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| classified entry → any finding list | The point at which an unclassified entry's title could escape as text instead of as a UID |
| the host's clock and locale → the anchors | A `Date` would let either move a weekday, which is the whole failure this phase was corrected to prevent |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-44-01 | Information disclosure | `ClassificationResult` findings | mitigate | every finding entry carries `uid` plus a reason code; the module holds no venue word and no example instance, and the acceptance grep asserts the absence of an alias literal |
| T-44-14 | Spoofing | civil-date arithmetic | mitigate | `isoWeekday` and `addDays` are computed from date parts; `new Date`, `getDay()` and every `Intl` path are asserted absent |
| T-44-16 | Tampering | the proposal path | mitigate | a date written in the file always wins and this module is never consulted for it; the night's and SunSet's listings return `not_derivable` by rule rather than by a computed guess |
| T-44-17 | Repudiation | a night with no next edition | mitigate | `awaiting_next_edition` is returned and named, so the correct behaviour is distinguishable from a gap; nothing invents a date for an edition that does not exist |
| T-44-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | accept | this phase installs no package |
</threat_model>
