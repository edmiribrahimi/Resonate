  <context>
    **On (1).** A plan row can carry a space at stage `mapped`, `verified` or `contacted`.
    Announcing publishes the night. If the venue travels with it, a space still in
    negotiation is named in public — and `venue-acquisition.md` says that closes the
    negotiation badly, and closes it for us: *finché una sede non è acquisita PER ISCRITTO,
    il suo nome non entra in nessun materiale.* Three shapes are available:
      - refuse the announcement outright until the stage is `acquired`;
      - allow it, and force `venue_secret = true` when the stage is anything else;
      - allow it and leave `venue_secret` to the existing event form.
    The second is the narrowest that does not block work, and it is the persona's reading —
    but forcing a flag is a decision about the venue secret, and the venue secret is
    monotone: `venue_reveal_sent` is a one-way switch and a mistake here is not reversible.

    **On (2).** Naming the venue in the confirmation makes the consequence concrete: the
    person pressing the button sees exactly what becomes public. Not naming it is one fewer
    place the word exists — and a dialog is screenshot-able. `44-UI-SPEC.md` §11.2 part 1
    renders whichever answer arrives; the sentence is written either way, only its content
    changes.

    **What has already been done rather than deciding for the owner:** the dialog's body is
    specified in four parts with part 1 as a slot, so neither answer changes its structure.
    And the announcement is a returned-value act, so a refusal on stage grounds is a
    branchable code rather than a redacted message.
