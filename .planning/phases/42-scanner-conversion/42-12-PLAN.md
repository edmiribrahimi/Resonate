<objective>
Close the phase's one falsifiable claim — *nothing behavioural moved* — with a
diff, and write down exactly which criteria are still open and who can close
them.

Purpose: success criterion 3 says every scanner behaviour is unchanged from
before the conversion. Ninety-nine class-string sites later, on a 3449-line file
that owns the offline queue, the torch, auto-return and undo, the only honest way
to say that is to re-run the capture taken before any of it and compare. **A
difference in any invariance block is a defect of the conversion until somebody
argues otherwise** — not a detail, and never something to reconcile by editing
the earlier record.

The second half is the part this project keeps getting right and must keep
getting right: **the diff does not close criterion 3.** The criterion says
*verified by running the door pass again on a device*. The record proves the
constants and the roads did not move; it never proves the behaviour did not. Two
of the three criteria end at a person's eye in a dark room, and ten rows of the
sampling map cannot be closed by any command in this repository. This plan writes
that down as a state, not as a caveat at the bottom.

Output: the after record beside the before, an evidence document with `file:line`
per requirement, and a procedures file that still reads `pending` wherever nobody
has looked yet.
</objective>
