<objective>
Stop the door stretching, without touching anything it decides.

Purpose: `ScannerClient.tsx` contains **no container maximum at all** — measured,
zero across the whole perimeter. On a phone the viewfinder card is about 310px
wide and the 280px decode box fits it; on a tablet the viewfinder stretches to
about 688px and on a desktop to about **1360px**, while the region actually
decoded stays 280px square in the middle. At the widest, the browser decodes an
area nearly **five times** the one that matters, fifteen times a second, and the
rest is a band of picture that serves nobody. *Stretches instead of centring* is
literal here, and it is true of the whole surface — header, pills, attendance
list — not only of the viewfinder.

The requirement and the success criterion are **not the same sentence**: RESP-05
says *the scanner centres*, criterion 2 says *the viewfinder centres*. Plan 42-04
decided to close both, by the route that keeps this inside colour, contrast and
type: the surface takes the same 1024px maximum every other default surface in
this product already carries — chosen, not invented, and reversible in one line —
and the viewfinder's own container takes a narrower one, because every pixel
beyond the decode region is video drawn for nothing.

The domain reason for 1024 rather than the widest available: the door is worked
**with one hand**, and on a tablet held sideways a thumb does not reach 1360px.
Centring at the number the rest of the product already reaches for is the same
reachability decision, not a symmetry.

Output: two maxima, both centred, and a decode configuration byte-identical to
the record taken before any of this began.
</objective>
