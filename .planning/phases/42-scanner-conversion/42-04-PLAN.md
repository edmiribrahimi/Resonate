<objective>
Decide every substitution before anyone opens the scanner, so that the conversion
is an application and not a sequence of judgements taken inside a 3449-line file.

Purpose: the perimeter carries **64 raw palette utilities and 42 legacy token
utilities** counted with the gate's own matcher (`42-RESEARCH.md` §4.1). Five of
them are chosen by a ternary rather than written as a literal, and one of them —
the third scan state — is drawn **twice**, in two files, with the same three SVG
paths. A conversion that decides targets while editing produces a surface where
the verdict says one colour and the history of the same scan says another, which
is the defect this phase was called to close, reintroduced by the plan meant to
close it.

Three decisions here are contested and each has a number attached. **The violet:**
`--sem-done` is close to the `purple-400` the scanner already uses for the guest
list and for the undone pill — 4,8 by one measurement and 8,0 by the other,
either way closer than amber ever was to yellow. **The ink:** the glyph is white
on all three fills today, and `globals.css:176-178` forbids white on a semantic
fill. **The Offline pill:** it is raw yellow in ten places on five different
features, and the gate written in plan 42-03 has to be able to read it.

And three things that are **not** colour arrive with the fence when it opens: 14
touch targets under the minimum, 18 prose citations of a file this phase deletes,
and a viewfinder that stretches to 1360px. Each needs a disposition written
**before** the first red run, because an exemption discovered on a red run is an
exemption nobody trusts.

Output: one table the conversion plans read, and four dispositions somebody
signed.
</objective>
