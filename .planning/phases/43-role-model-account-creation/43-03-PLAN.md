<objective>
The constraint of ROLE-02 has a price, the ROADMAP names it, and this plan is
where it is paid: *"the harness must keep the ability to seed those states, or
this phase buys a real rule at the price of the only net that has already caught
something."*

Four of the nine seeded personas become unrepresentable when plan 43-06 lands.
The harness does not degrade quietly — it throws, in three places, and
`seed.mjs:317-324` forbids by name the repair somebody would reach for under time
pressure (*"investigate the seed, never lower the requirement"*). So the seam has
to be built deliberately, and it is built **before** the constraint exists rather
than after it breaks something.

The mechanism is not a choice between five options. `43-RESEARCH.md` measured all
five in a `postgres:17.6` container: a CHECK cannot be `DEFERRABLE`; `NOT VALID`
still refuses every new violating insert; neither `SECURITY DEFINER`, nor
superuser, nor `session_replication_role = 'replica'` bypasses it. With a CHECK,
**drop-and-restore is the only route and the restore must be `NOT VALID`**,
because four rows violate the predicate by the time the `finally` runs.

Purpose: keep the only automated detector this repository owns, and give ROLE-02
its only automated detector at the same time.
Output: one file, one relaxation, three assertions, proved by mutation against a
scratch migration.
</objective>
