<objective>
`ACCT-05` is already structurally true and that changes what this plan is for.
The attendance insert has no role branch and no status branch, so a `staff`
account scanning its membership card produces a row identical in shape to any
member's, today, with no new code. It is **counted**.

What it is not is **readable**. Target venues hold 150–300, each staff account is
a permanent free entry, and after two seasons that is a standing block of seats
given away months in advance — *"uncounted entries make the night's numbers wrong
exactly where they are relied on."* To answer *how many of tonight's 180 were free
staff entries* the entries have to be distinguishable, and the three ways to do
that are not equivalent:

- **Joining to `profiles.role` at read time is wrong, and subtly.** `role` is
  mutable. A staff account demoted next month makes last month's night report a
  different number. The repository already states the principle for
  `ticket_refunds`: evidence that must outlive the row it names is denormalised
  at write time, deliberately not a foreign key.
- **`door_scan_events.subject_type` cannot see it.** It tells a membership scan
  from a ticket scan, not a staff member's card from an approved member's.
- **A denormalised column is correct and cheap** — online. Offline it is not
  cheap, and D-17 takes that cost deliberately: the roster carries the role, the
  device holds it, and the marker recorded is the one taken at the door.

This plan takes the online half and the payload. Plan 43-13 takes the device.

Purpose: ACCT-05, made readable rather than merely true.
Output: one column, one word in a hot-path select, one field in the roster.
</objective>

Nullable, **no default**, **no foreign key**, **no CHECK** tying it to the role
list. Write all four decisions with their reasons:

- *nullable and no default* — the existing-rows paragraph: rows written before

  comped guest is also a free entry and is not staff.

same commit, and note there — as plan 43-07 noted for the register — that no
Supabase client in this repository is parameterised with `Database`, so this
name is checked by nothing at any call site.
