<objective>
This is the only part of the phase that touches the door, and it inherits
`checkin-offline.md` whole: the door has no network, refusing a valid guest is
worse than admitting a duplicate, and **an upgrade that strands a queued scan is
unacceptable**.

`43-PATTERNS.md` reports the analog as **absent**, and that is a finding rather
than a gap to fill by approximation. The one upgrade this file has ever contained
is guarded by `if (oldVersion >= 3) return;`, never branches on `oldVersion`, and
its body unconditionally deletes the `attendees` and `pendingCheckins` stores. Run
that for a v3 → v4 hop and it destroys queued admissions — rows for people who
paid, on a device that is offline right now and cannot be audited. A v4 has to
serve **two** entry paths, a device at v2 and a device at v3, and the existing
code gives no shape for either.

`.planning/STATE.md` warns that the IndexedDB upgrade must be exercised **before
the first real night**, and notes why that is safe to demand now: no staff phone
holds a database with real check-ins, because no event is published. D-17 puts
that exercise inside this phase, and the checkpoint at the end of this plan is it.

Purpose: the device half of ACCT-05, taken without costing anyone their queue.
Output: one version bump that deletes nothing, one field carried forward, one
upgrade exercised on a real device.
</objective>

| the roster on a device → who can be admitted offline | An unknown membership code is refused offline; the roster is the whole of the door's knowledge |
