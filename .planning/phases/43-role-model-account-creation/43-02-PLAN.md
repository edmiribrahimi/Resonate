<objective>
Build the detector before the thing it detects.

`npm run verify:capabilities` today measures four declarations of the capability
**catalogue** and says so in its own words: *"It does not mean a capability is
granted to the right roles … `private.role_capabilities` is not read here at
all."* That sentence is why a wrong grant row has **no automated detector** in
this repository (`32-CARRY-FORWARD.md` trap D-32-L, re-verified).

Plan 43-05 inserts `staff`'s grant rows. Plan 43-07 mints a ninth capability.
Both are exactly the shape of change the missing check would catch, and the one
mistake that matters most — a refusal expressed as a row rather than as an
absence — would **grant** every refused capability, because the resolver's
`EXISTS` never looks at a `granted` column (`20260807000000_capability_model.sql:209-216`).

This plan adds a fifth side: the cross product of roles and capabilities, every
pair either a declared grant with its `requires_approved`, or a declared refusal
with no row at all. It is green today, before anything changes — which is the
point of writing it first.

Purpose: convert D-02 from a convention into a mechanism, and give D-06 a
tripwire.
Output: one script, five sides, proved by mutation in both directions.
</objective>
