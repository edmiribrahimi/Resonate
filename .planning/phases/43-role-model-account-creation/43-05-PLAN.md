<objective>
The fourth role lands.

Two facts govern the shape and both were measured. **`role` is constrained in two
places**, not one: `20260224_rbac_migration.sql:14-15` is the one production
enforces, and `20260807000000_capability_model.sql:121` guards
`private.role_capabilities`. Widening only the first produces `staff` accounts
holding **zero** capabilities — including their own membership card, which is the
entire purpose of the role. And **editing `supabase/schema.sql` alone changes
nothing at all**: the container builds from that file as it stood at the initial
commit, pinned by blob hash, then applies every migration.

The refusals matter as much as the grant. D-02 requires that "considered and
refused" be distinguishable from "forgotten", and the trap is specific: a
`granted = false` row **grants** the capability, because
`private.has_capability`'s `EXISTS` matches on `(role, capability)` alone. So the
refusal is the **absence** of a row, declared in `scripts/verify-capabilities.mjs`
by plan 43-02, and asserted there.

Purpose: ROLE-01, with the six refusals visible as decisions.
Output: one migration, four TypeScript-side edits, one applied schema, one clean
container comparison.
</objective>
