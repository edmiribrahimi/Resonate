<objective>
This plan exists because one window closes permanently later in this phase, and
three unknowns are cheaper to settle now than to discover inside a migration.

**The window.** `32-HUMAN-UAT.md` M-12 — the check the owner named "run this one
first" — requires an account with `role = organizer` and `status = pending` to
sign in and reach `/admin/scanner`. D-04's constraint makes that state
unrepresentable. After plan 43-06 the check cannot be run by anyone, for any
reason, ever again. Its result is what D-06 needs on the day somebody proposes
removing `door.operate`'s `requires_approved = false` as tidying.

**The unknowns.** Whether any live row already violates `role ⇒ approved`
(because a `NOT VALID` add would *freeze* such a row permanently — measured, no
column of it could ever be updated again); what the two live role constraints are
actually called; and whether a `23514` reaches application code as a code rather
than as prose, because every "the refusal must be observable" decision in this
phase rests on it.

Purpose: nothing after this plan is written against an assumption where a
measurement was available.
Output: M-12 closed with a date, and `43-MEASUREMENTS.md` holding four measured
facts.
</objective>
