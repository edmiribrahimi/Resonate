<objective>
Six acts change who somebody is, and today none of them leaves a trace beyond the
mutated row. This plan routes all of them through the register of plan 43-07 and
makes their failures visible.

Three things happen at once and they belong together, because each is meaningless
without the others.

**The register write is not a second call.** `.update()` followed by an insert
cannot be atomic across two PostgREST requests, and a mutation that succeeded
while its record failed is precisely the untraced act D-11 forbids. Every act
becomes one `.rpc()` to `record_membership_act`, which does both inside one
transaction.

**The ceiling moves, once, deliberately.** D-21: `updateMemberRole` calls
`verifyMaster()` today, so *"an organizer may promote a staff member to
organizer"* is a change this phase makes, not a behaviour it inherits. The file's
own comment block explains why the two gates must not be merged — merging onto
`STAFF_MANAGE` hands every organizer the power to change a role, merging onto
`MASTER_MANAGE` takes approve and reject from every organizer. So the widening is
surgical: role changes within the ceiling move to the organizer gate;
`deactivateMember` and `reactivateMember` stay master-only.

**A refusal becomes a value.** The constraint of plan 43-06 fires on a path
nobody tested, on the day it fires. Next redacts a Server Action's message in a
production build, so a caller that branches on `err.message` works in `next dev`
and stops working where it matters. And there is **no error tracking** in this
product: a log line reaches nobody.

Purpose: ACCT-04, the organizer half of ACCT-01, and the observable half of
ROLE-02.
Output: one file, eight exported acts, one shape.
</objective>
