## How this discussion actually went

Four gray areas were presented with the alternatives priced. The owner answered
both questions with **"expert persona decides best approach based on deep
research"** — the same delegation shape as Phase 41's, where D-41-02 was priced,
handed over, and returned as *"decidi tu"*.

So no area was discussed turn by turn, and **no `DISCUSS-CHECKPOINT.json` was
written**: there was no partially-completed area to resume. What followed the
delegation was measurement, not conversation, and every decision in CONTEXT.md
carries the figure that produced it.

**One thing the delegation did not cover, and CONTEXT.md says so:** the owner
delegated the *approach*. If the approach turns out to need a file under the auth
group or the payment callback opened, that is a halt and a fresh impact analysis,
not a call made at execution time (D-41.1-04).

## Area 1 — the clearance on the four focus routes (DEF-41-08)

| Option | Description | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Move the clearance to the layout that mounts the navigation | Removes the reachability. Round 5 priced it as Critical — edits under `access-gating` and `ticketing-payments` | ✓ (in a form that is neither) |
| Widen the gate's scan to every climbed ancestor | Only measures it; reddens a correct file the moment a wrapper legitimately reserves a column | |
| Both | | |
| Look at the four screens first, decide after | | partly — H41-4 is still owed regardless |

**Choice:** delegated.
**What the measurement changed.** The premise that made this Critical does not
hold. 13 navigation mount sites were counted; only **two** mount the responsive
form that produces the 224px column, and eleven mount `MobileNav`, bar-locked at
every width. `globals.css:300-305` declares the column clearance ambiently on
`:root` at `md` and up — **a statement already false for eleven of thirteen mount
sites**, and false for every nav-less route. Emptying the ambient default and
letting the two declaring sites declare it touches three files, two of them
already converted, **none under `(auth)` or `payment/callback`**.

**Alternative examined and rejected:** `:root:has([data-app-nav])`, which would
have expressed the invariant with zero mount-site edits. It is Baseline since
December 2023 and Tailwind v4's own floor already sits above it, so support was
never the objection — **MDN's explicit warning against anchoring `:has()` to
`:root` or `body`** is, and this product re-renders a live attendee list on a
phone at a door.
