<objective>
Stop `--nav-inset-inline-start` from being ambient. The two sites that mount the
responsive navigation form declare the 224px column on the wrapper around their
content; `:root` keeps the top-level zero and loses the media-query override; the
toast stops depending on a value it is mounted above; and check E stops freezing a
shape and starts asserting a pairing.

Purpose: `globals.css:300-305` declares the column clearance for **thirteen**
navigation mount sites of which only **two** have a column. That statement is
already false for eleven of them and for every route that mounts no navigation at
all. Nothing breaks visibly today only because the eleven hard-code their own
bottom padding and never read the variable. With the default at zero, **the
clearance becomes structurally unable to reach a route that mounts no
navigation** — which is `DEF-41-08`'s resolution.

Output: the declaration moved, the toast's regression closed in the same plan
that would have introduced it, and a gate that fails in both directions.
</objective>
