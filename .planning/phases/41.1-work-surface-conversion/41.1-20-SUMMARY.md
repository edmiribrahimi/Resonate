# Phase 41.1 Plan 20: The Venue-Reveal Knot Summary

The confirmation that stands between an operator and an irreversible send is now
the platform's modal, its copy is byte-identical across **44 user-visible
literals**, and **not one line carrying any of the six reveal needles moved in
the panel**. The conversion is a strengthening — but **not the strengthening the
phase predicted**, and the difference is the most important thing in this file.

---

## 1. Impact analysis — written before the diff

**Classification: Critical.** `venue-secrecy.md`: a location can only be
revealed, never re-hidden. The mail has gone, the screenshot exists, the address
is in somebody's camera roll. There is no rollback, so an error here is not a
defect to fix later — it is a fact.

**Domain:** `venue-secrecy` primary; `comms-analytics` and `ticketing-payments`
secondary, because the reveal mails ticket and RSVP holders.

**What was opened:** the reveal panel and the reveal confirmation, and **only**
those two. A file-level split is not available: the closure walk passes *through*
the panel into the dialog, and the panel is what the event edit surface imports.

**What was read and not edited:** the reveal server action, the reveal library,
the reveal email template and the cron tree. They were read so that a rendering
change could be told from a behaviour change. `git status --porcelain` at the end
of this plan is **empty**, and the only two files in the plan's commits are the
two declared.

**Cross-domain impact, and what it produced:**

- *Venue secrecy.* The panel decides whether the manual reveal is offered at all,
  and the dialog is the last thing before an irreversible send. **Zero diff lines
  touch any conditional that decides either** — proved below, and proved by
  absence rather than by inspection.
- *Communications.* A count that feeds an irreversible act must keep declaring
  whether it could measure at all, not only how many. The unavailable state
  survives with its own words, unchanged; so does the trace's third state.
- *Access and gating.* Re-hiding is a master-only path decided inside the
  database function, which reads the role itself. **No second verdict on the role
  was added, and none was removed.**

**The three stop conditions:** none was crossed. No status transition, no refund
amount, no idempotency key, no webhook path, no progressivo. The monotone guard
is discussed at length below because it is the one this plan is about.

## 2. The focus target, before and after — four lines, each with its evidence

This is the plan's centre, and **the plan's own premise turned out to be
wrong**. It is recorded here in the shape this phase uses for a reversal,
because a withdrawn claim without its measurement reads as a slip.

| # | Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which control did the DOM place first? | **Cancel.** There was no close control at all, so Cancel was the first focusable descendant of the modal | pre-conversion file, the cancel control at `:423-432`, the confirming control at `:434` |
| 2 | Which control carried the autofocus prop? | **Cancel** | pre-conversion file `:426` |
| 3 | What did the browser actually focus? | **Cancel — and it landed.** The prop is inert, but the file's own open effect called `.focus()` on the cancel ref **immediately after `showModal()`**, which is exactly the moment that is not overwritten | pre-conversion file `:235-253`, the call at `:249`; the inertness measured at `Dialog.tsx:117-148` out of the installed `react-dom@19` |
| 4 | What does it focus now? | **Cancel**, by the initial-focus marker the primitive queries and focuses imperatively after `showModal()` — one mechanism, the inert prop gone rather than kept beside it | `RevealVenueDialog.tsx:432`; `Dialog.tsx:246-262` |

### The correction, stated as a correction

**D-41.1-24 and `41.1-PATTERNS.md` §5.3 say the focus placement here was
*"unspecified in practice"*.** That is right about the prop and **wrong about
this file**. The pattern map read the attribute at `:426` and stopped; the
imperative call sixteen lines above the render, at `:249`, was not read. Cancel
**was** focused before this plan.

This is precisely the failure the prompt for this plan named in the opposite
direction — *"it already focused cancel" is exactly the claim that turned out to
be false* — and it is worth saying that the discipline caught it in the direction
nobody was watching: the argument **for** the work was the one that did not
survive contact with the file.

### So what actually got tighter, and it is not nothing

Three things, and each is checkable:

1. **Two mechanisms became one.** An inert prop sitting beside a live imperative
   call is a decoy: the next reader cannot tell which one is load-bearing, and
   deleting the wrong one would have silently removed the focus. The analog
   already records that exact decision (`RetireFormatDialog.tsx:283-284`).
2. **The focus became VISIBLE.** The pre-conversion controls were hand-rolled
   buttons declaring **no focus expression at all** — so a person opening the
   confirmation could not see which control held the focus, and *"Cancel is
   focused"* was true and unobservable. Both controls are now `Button`, which
   carries the shared focus expression at a 2px offset. **This is the change that
   turns row 1 of the human procedure from an assertion into an observation.**
3. **The refusal left the scroller.** It used to be a tinted box inside the
   scrolling body, so on a long consequence list it could appear below the fold.
   The primitive renders it outside the scroller, always on screen, with its
   alert role intact.

**The sentence the monotone guard needs:** the reveal is at least as hard to
trigger after this conversion as before, and harder in the one respect a person
can check. Nothing shortens the path between opening the panel and sending: same
number of steps, same trigger conditions, same confirmation, same words.

## 5. The one reversal, and why it goes the safe way

The confirming control now takes the **destructive rung**. This file previously
refused that rung in writing, and the refusal is **quoted in place rather than
deleted**:

> *"NOT the destructive red … revealing does not destroy, it publishes. Red on
> this surface is the colour of a refusal box, and borrowing it here would say
> 'this might break something' when the true sentence is 'this reaches people and
> cannot be recalled'. The brake is the number in the paragraph above, not a
> hue."*

Two things overrule it, and both are written into the file beside the quotation:

1. **`41-UI-SPEC.md` §11 names this dialog by name** as one of the three
   destructive confirmations and fixes the rung for all three. A house rule a
   surface opts out of per file is not a rule.
2. **The reason it argued from no longer holds.** It argued that red on this
   surface is the colour of a refusal box — and the refusal is no longer a box.
   It is the primitive's status region in the critical semantic ink, so the
   confirming control and a refusal are now told apart by position and role.

What does not change is the sentence the old comment was defending: **the brake
on this act is the number of people**, and the rung is not asked to carry it.
And the direction is the safe one under a monotone guard — a heavier confirming
control makes the act harder to press by accident, never easier.

Taking a night back to secret keeps a lighter treatment: it publishes nothing and
sends nothing, so it does not borrow the destructive rung. It takes the primary
rung so that it cannot be mistaken for the cancel beside it — the same asymmetry
the analog draws between retiring and restoring.

**Relation to D-41.1-25 and D-41.1-29:** neither is touched. Those govern
*outcome tones on a status*, and this plan adds none: the panel's four
raw-palette sentences become semantic **ink**, one per cause, and no status
anywhere grades an outcome by hue. D-41.1-29's measured 1.23 : 1 is the reason
nobody should try; it did not have to be relied on, because nothing here needed
two fills.

**D-41.1-26 and D-41.1-27:** no navigation control was introduced, so no
`Button href` question arose; no typographic measure and no container maximum was
written on either file, so check D's vocabulary was not needed.
