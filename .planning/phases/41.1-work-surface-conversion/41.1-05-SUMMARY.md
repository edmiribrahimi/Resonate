## What changed, in the order it matters

### 1. The ambient claim is gone, and it was already false

`@media (min-width: 48rem)` on `:root`. That statement was true of **two** of the
**thirteen** navigation mount sites and false of the other eleven, which reach
the navigation through the bar-locked wrapper and have no column at any width —
and false of every route that mounts no navigation at all.

The media-query declaration of the inline axis is deleted. **The top-level
declaration at zero stays**, because deleting *that* is the reverse edit the
file's own docblock warns about: `findBlock()` in `verify-tokens.mjs:465-484`

referenced elsewhere in the file makes check A go red on a correct file — Phase
40 paid for that once. Measured after the edit: `verify-tokens.mjs` exits 0,
prints seven checks, *31 declared in `:root` · 23 exposed · 8 under check D*,

## Deviations from plan

### 1. `[Rule 2 — missing critical assertion]` A third failure direction in check E

**Found during:** task 3, while writing the declaring-side matcher.

**Issue:** the plan specifies matching the property inside a bracketed arbitrary
value **at the `md` variant**. A matcher anchored on that variant treats a
declaration written at any *other* variant — or at none — as **absent**. So
`[‹PROP›:14rem]` with no variant, which reserves 224px of
leading edge on a phone where the navigation is a bar at the bottom, would have
made a mounting file look like a non-declaring one in direction 2 and a
non-mounting file look clean in direction 1. That is a hole in the direction that
prints a tick, which is the shape this whole plan exists to stop.

**Fix:** the match is value- and variant-agnostic; the tier is asserted
separately, and a declaration off the tier is a **failure** naming the file, the
line, the token and the variants found.

**Files modified:** `scripts/verify-conversion.mjs`. **Commit:** `ed50e53`.

### 2. `[Rule 3 — the plan's criterion could not be met as written]` Zero occurrences in `ToastContainer.tsx`

The plan asks the docblock to record the loss *and* asks
`grep -c "nav-inset-inline-start" src/components/toast/ToastContainer.tsx` to
return **0**. Both hold only if the docblock names the property by description.
It does — *"the inline-axis navigation clearance property, the leading-edge
counterpart of the block-axis one this file still reads"* — with the reason
stated in the file, and that reason is the file's own precedent about its
previous literal, not an improvisation.

### 3. `[interpretation, not a fix]` "Replace check E" read as "replace what check E is about"

The plan says *replace check E*; its acceptance criteria require the two frozen
digests to survive unchanged, and RESEARCH §3.6 says they *stay valid*. Those two
cannot both hold if the frozen-shape assertions are deleted, since nothing else
reads those constants.

**Taken:** the pairing is added as check E's new half and is what now carries
`DEF-41-08`. The frozen-shape assertions are kept — not because they are trusted
to catch `DEF-41-08` (five rounds say they are not), but because they guard a
**different** property the pairing does not cover: that `PageShell`'s focus form
declares no clearance, and that the shell has not stopped reading either property
altogether. Deleting them would have removed a live protection and orphaned two
constants the plan requires to remain.

**This is the one judgement in this plan a reviewer should look at first**, and it
is written here rather than left in a diff.
