<impact_analysis>
## Written before the diff — required by D-41.1-19 and by the Response Gate

**Classification: Critical.** Not because of size. Because this form writes a
night: its format, its series, its venue link, and **its venue secrecy flag**.

**Domain:** `venue-secrecy` primary for the secrecy field; `supabase-data` and
`access-gating` for what the form submits; `nextjs-architecture` for the file
itself; `production-calendar` for the format and series fields.

**What is opened:** `src/components/events/EventForm.tsx` and nothing else.

**What is not opened:** the five components it imports — the two creation modals,
the two autocomplete inputs and the venue profile prompt — which belong to plan
41.1-19, running in the same wave on a disjoint file set. The server actions it
calls are read but never edited.

**Cross-domain impact, stated before the diff:**

- *Venue secrecy.* The form carries the secrecy flag. `venue-secrecy.md` treats a
  location as something that can only be revealed, never re-hidden. A conversion
  that made the flag easier to toggle, or that surfaced an address field earlier
  in the layout, would be a change to a monotone guard wearing a styling change's
  clothes.
- *The production calendar.* The form carries the format and series fields and a
  night's number. A series progressivo is already on a poster; nothing here
  renumbers, re-sorts or re-derives one, and the number field's optionality is
  behaviour that stays exactly as it is.
- *Access and gating.* The form is drawn for roles whose write may still be
  refused. Every refusal path stays reachable and every refusal keeps its own
  sentence.
- *Accessibility.* Three toggle tracks are drawn at 24px against a 44px floor and
  each spells its own focus suppression. That is the finding the `Switch`
  primitive exists to close, and closing it here is the third of its four sites.

**A recorded todo that this plan does NOT fold.** There is an open project todo
about the secrecy flag leaving no trace when it is unticked. It is a **behaviour**
finding, not a conversion finding, and folding it would put a non-visual change
inside a conversion commit — the exact shape this repository has recorded as how
a contract decision gets made where nobody reviews it. **This plan must not make
it worse, and must not fix it.** If the conversion reveals anything further about
it, that goes in the SUMMARY as a finding.

## The three stop conditions, verbatim

1. **`venue_reveal_sent` is monotone.** Work may only make the reveal **harder**
   to trigger, never easier. Nothing in this file may lower the effort required
   to set or unset the secrecy flag, and nothing may move an address field
   earlier in the reading order.
2. **A payment reaching `completed` corrects forward.** No conversion changes a
   status transition, a refund amount, an idempotency key or a webhook path.
   Nothing in this file reaches one; asserted by grep.
3. **A series progressivo is already on a poster.** No conversion renumbers,
   re-sorts or re-derives one.

**A plan that finds it cannot convert without crossing one of those halts and
returns to the owner.** That is written here so it is refused rather than
discovered.
</impact_analysis>
