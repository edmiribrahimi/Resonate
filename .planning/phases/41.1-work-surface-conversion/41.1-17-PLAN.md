## Written before the diff — required by D-41.1-19 and by the Response Gate

**Classification: Critical.** These files render a control that moves money out of
the business. The persona's own rule applies: *measure twice, cut once* — the
approach is stated here and the diff follows it, not the other way round.

**Domain:** `ticketing-payments` primary; `nextjs-architecture` and
`access-gating` secondary. `meta-gates.md`'s monotone guard on a payment reaching
completion binds every line.

**What is opened:** `src/components/admin/RefundDialog.tsx` (181 lines, 28 class
attributes) and
`src/app/(admin)/admin/events/[id]/tickets/RefundActions.tsx` (165 lines, 19).

**What is not opened, and must not be:** `src/app/(public)/tickets/refund-actions.ts`,
`src/emails/refund-approved.tsx`, `src/emails/refund-rejected.tsx`, the SumUp
webhook route, and every cron under `src/app/api/cron/`. Those carry **zero**
class attributes; they are in the closure and therefore in the gate's scan, but a
conversion does not edit them. The email palette is deferred by an explicit owner
decision and is not this phase's.

**Cross-domain impact:**
- *Ticketing and payments:* the confirmation's copy, its focus target and its
  button variants all sit on the path to a refund. Changing any of them changes
  how easy the refund is to trigger.
- *Access and gating:* the control is drawn for a role that may not be allowed to
  press it; the refusal path must stay reachable and readable.
- *Check-in:* a refund issued after a night began is accounting and never a door
  event. Nothing here touches that boundary, and the SUMMARY says so.

**Why the conversion makes the guard tighter rather than looser.** The house rule
(`41-UI-SPEC.md` §11, built at `RetireFormatDialog.tsx`) puts Cancel first in the
DOM, first in the tab order, and gives it the initial-focus marker that the
primitive honours **imperatively after the modal opens** — because React's
autofocus prop is skipped by the attribute writer and never reaches the DOM at
all. Any hand-rolled overlay today has no focus trap, no escape and no background
inertness; the primitive supplies all three by specification.

## The three stop conditions, verbatim

1. **`venue_reveal_sent` is monotone.** Work may only make the reveal **harder**
   to trigger, never easier — and changing a confirmation's focus target, its
   button variant or its copy *is* touching that guard. Nothing in these two files
   reaches the reveal; asserted by grep, not assumed.
2. **A payment reaching `completed` corrects forward.** **No conversion changes a
   status transition, a refund amount, an idempotency key or a webhook path.** A
   plan that finds it cannot convert without doing so **halts and returns to the
   owner.**
3. **A series progressivo is already on a poster.** No conversion renumbers,
   re-sorts or re-derives one.
</impact_analysis>
