## Summary

The conversion itself is **small and almost perfectly partitionable**. Walking
the 21 unconverted work pages with `verify-conversion.mjs`'s own import-closure
semantics gives **116 non-spine files, of which 74 carry a `className`** — and
of those 74, **only seven are reached by more than one unconverted work page**.
Those seven fall into exactly **two clusters** (the event form's six, and
`RefundActions.tsx`). Every other page owns its whole visual closure alone. The
joint-obligation failure `DEF-41-03` records twice is therefore avoidable by a
wave cut that keeps two pairs of pages together, and by nothing else.

**The hard part is not the pages. It is three things the pages sit on.**

1. **Six visual files in a work page's closure are declared 41.2's, by name.**
   `EventForm.tsx` (1 669 lines), `RefundDialog.tsx`, `RefundActions.tsx`,
   `RevealVenueDialog.tsx`, `VenueRevealPanel.tsx` and `EventQRCode.tsx` (shared
   with the public bar). CONTEXT.md says both *"the 21 unconverted work pages
   and everything their import closures reach"* is in scope **and** that the
   event form, the refunds and `RevealVenueDialog` are 41.2's. Both cannot be
   true. Criterion 1 — *"every page under the work surface has converted
   whole"* — cannot be satisfied for `/events/new`, `/events/[id]/edit`,
   `/finance`, `/events/[id]/sales`, `/events/[id]/tickets` and
   `/events/[id]/drinks` without opening them.

2. **D-41.1-01 as written moves a live defect onto this phase's own surface.**
   `ToastProvider` is mounted at `src/app/layout.tsx:120` — the **root** layout,
   outside every route group and outside the wrapper the decision puts the
   clearance on. `ToastContainer.tsx:64` reads `--nav-inset-inline-start`. Under
   D-41.1-01 it resolves to `0px` at every width on every route, so the toast
   stops clearing the 224 px column on the work surface — and the work surface
   is where `useToast`'s only consumer lives (`GuestListClient.tsx:73`, on
   `/events/[id]/guest-list`, a page **this** phase converts). Today the ambient
   value is wrong on 11 of 13 mount sites and right on 2; after the change it is
   right on 11 and wrong on the 2 that matter here. The fix is a fourth file,
   `ToastContainer.tsx`, which is spine and in territory — so the **Structured**
   classification survives — but it is a decision, not a diff.

3. **The `REMAINING` lists are single files that every conversion plan wants to
   edit.** D-41.1-15 partitions *files*; a `REMAINING` list is one file touched
   by every plan that pays an entry. D-41.1-16's post-wave reconciliation task
   is the resolution and should be named as the **single owner of every gate-list
   edit in its wave** — otherwise the phase's own parallelism rule is unsatisfiable.

**Primary recommendation:** cut six waves — the shared stripper alone first
(D-41.1-08), then the clearance and the group tag, then three waves of pages
partitioned by the shared-component table below, then the two knots and the
shim's second step last — and put the boundary conflict in §7 to the owner
**before** planning, because it decides whether wave 5 exists in this phase at all.

### 1.4 The wave cut, and the rule that produced it

**The rule.** *Two plans in the same wave may not both declare a file that
carries a `className`. A file two plans would both need is either (a) converted
in an earlier wave, (b) assigned to exactly one plan, or (c) the two pages are
merged into one plan.* This is D-41.1-15, made operational against the table above.

**And one extension the rule needs, which D-41.1-15 does not yet state.** A
`REMAINING` list is **one file** edited by every plan that pays an entry, so
under the rule as written no two conversion plans could ever share a wave. The
resolution is already half-written in D-41.1-16: **the post-wave reconciliation
task is the single owner of every gate-script edit in its wave.** Conversion
plans convert; they do not delete entries. The reconciliation task re-runs the
gates, reads the `STALE` lines the gates already print, and makes every deletion
in one commit. Recommend the planner state this explicitly — it is the
difference between a rule that can be followed and one that cannot.

### 4.3 The extraction, proposed and proved

**Where it lives.** `scripts/lib/comments.mjs`. A subdirectory rather than a
sibling `.mjs`, because `scripts/` currently holds only executables plus two

**Exported surface** — three names and nothing else:

```

liveLinesFrom(rawLines)    -> { lines, unterminated }    // unterminated: { kind, lineNo } | null
```

`verify-dialogs.mjs:1247` currently feeds single-line probe strings through

not — the multi-line state lives in `liveLines`, and the self-check is
structurally unable to exercise it. With `liveLinesFrom(rawLines)` exported, the
probe rows become line arrays and the self-check can see a fix to either
multi-line shape.

**The design, proved.** `scratchpad/merged-candidate.mjs` implements
family B's span logic + family D's block state + CR-01's terminating-line span +

Three things make it right where the six previous rounds were not:

1. **The closer is a run-time-assembled regex**, `\*\/\s*\}`, not the exact token

### 4.5 The mutation harness (D-41.1-09)

## 6. The shim's removal (D-41.1-17)

### 6.1 What is there now

`src/app/(admin)/admin/(work)/layout.tsx:119-152`. Two nested elements around
`{children}`:

- **outer** (`:130`): `ps-[var(--nav-inset-inline-start)]` — takes the real
  clearance, 0 on a phone, 224 px from `md` up **because `:root` says so today**.
- **inner** (`:143-147`): `style={{ "--nav-inset-inline-start": "0px" }}` —
  redeclares the variable as zero for its whole subtree, so a converted page
  rendering `PageShell` (which reads the same variable at `PageShell.tsx:160`)
  adds **nothing on top**.

Its exit is already written into its own docblock (`:83-87`): *"removed by the
plan that converts the last page under this layout, at which point `PageShell`
owns the clearance alone."*

### 6.2 The three states, written out

**State 0 — today.** Outer pads from the ambient. Inner zeroes it. Converted
pages (`/admin/formats`, `/admin/members`, `/admin/members/register`) render
`PageShell`, which reads `0px` and adds nothing. Unconverted pages render their
own markup with no leading padding and are cleared by the outer element.
**Correct for both, which is the whole point of the shim.**

**State 1 — after wave 1A, while pages are still unconverted.** `:root` no longer
declares `14rem` at `md`. The layout must now **declare** the clearance as well
as apply it, because nothing above it does. The two elements stay two:

- outer: declares the column clearance at the `md` tier **and** applies it as
  leading padding — one element carrying the arbitrary-property utility and the
  `ps-*` utility together.
- inner: unchanged, still zeroing the variable for the subtree, so a converted
  page's `PageShell` adds nothing.

The declaration is what check E's new pairing (§3.6) asserts, and it is what
makes this file one of the two members of the responsive-mount set.

**State 2 — after the last page converts (plan 5A).** The inner element goes and
the outer stops padding. One element remains, carrying **only** the declaration:

```
<div className="md:[--nav-inset-inline-start:14rem]">
  <StaffNav … form="strip" />
  {children}
</div>
```

Every page below now renders `PageShell`, which reads `14rem` at `md` and applies
it itself — D-41-03's original design, reached at last. **`PageShell.tsx` is not
edited at any point**, which is what keeps `verify-conversion`'s two frozen
digests valid.

> *Note per DEF-41-01: the utility above is a complete, valid Tailwind arbitrary
> property, written whole rather than abbreviated because the shim's own comment
> (`layout.tsx:138-141`) records that an abbreviated one emits a malformed rule
> and a build warning. `.planning/` is scanned by Tailwind's source detection, so
> this document now makes that rule a live candidate; the emitted rule has **zero
> consumers under `src/`** until the plan lands and is inert until then. Recorded
> rather than left for whoever next greps the emitted CSS.*

### 6.3 What breaks if the two steps are done in the wrong order

| Wrong order | What a person sees |
|---|---|
| **The shim's inner element is removed before the last page converts** | Every already-converted page under `(work)` gets **448 px** of leading padding at ≥768 px: 224 from the layout's outer element, 224 more from its own `PageShell`. Content squeezed into 320 px on a 768 px tablet. Loud, and caught by the first H41-1 |
| **The shim's outer padding is removed before the last page converts** | Every **unconverted** page sits under the 224 px column at ≥768 px — the first ~224 px of every row hidden behind navigation. This is the failure the shim was written to prevent, reintroduced at the end |
| **The declaration is added to the layout but not to `gallery/page.tsx` (wave 1A)** | `/gallery` — already converted, `wide` — loses its column clearance and its content slides under the column at ≥768 px. Loud, and **check E's new pairing catches it**, which is the argument for putting the assertion in the same plan |
| **The declaration is added and `ToastContainer` is not addressed (§3.4)** | Nothing at all, until somebody uses the guest list on a tablet. That is the quiet one, and it is why plan 2E's H41-1 must raise a toast |

**Ordering rule for the planner, stated once:** *the layout may stop padding only
in the same commit that declares the last page converted, and may not stop
declaring at all.*

## 9. Common pitfalls

### P1 — A `REMAINING` count that falls because the gate stopped looking

**What goes wrong:** a debt number drops, a reader concludes work happened.
**Why:** four distinct mechanisms have produced it in this phase family already
— a never-measured `Map` (DEF-41-03), a fence match (WR-02), an unguarded
walk-membership check (CR-03), and a comment stripper going blind inside a file
(CR-02, measured live on `RefundDialog.tsx:60` with the gate printing *"converted;
remove this entry"* and exiting 0).
**How to avoid:** D-41.1-16's reconciliation task diffs **entries**, not counts,
and re-runs the gates against the tree.
**Warning sign:** a count moved in a commit that touched no product file.

### P2 — A joint obligation between two plans in one wave

**What goes wrong:** two plans each satisfy their own contract; the work between
them belongs to neither afterwards.
**Why:** wave parallelism, and nothing in the workflow looks for it.
**How to avoid:** §1.3's shared-component table, D-41.1-15, and the reconciliation
task as sole owner of gate-list edits (§1.4).
**Warning sign:** a plan's summary says *"this is the call site that adopts it
when it lands"*.

### P3 — A gate that goes red on a correct file

**What goes wrong:** it gets switched off, and then it guards nothing —
`verify-media-strip.mjs:51-62`, cited five times across this file family.
**Live exposures right now:** §1.5 (`/admin`, no `PageShell`), §4.2's four
FALSE-RED shapes, and DEF-41-04's typographic measure.
**How to avoid:** write the exemption **before** the gate's first run (§0 rule 3,
D-41-16). §1.5 and §2.5 are two exemptions that must be decided at planning time.

### P4 — A conversion that changes what a query returns

**What goes wrong:** a visual plan touches a `select`, a capability key, a filter
or a column, and the change reaches access or money.
**Why:** the converted files also contain the queries.
**How to avoid:** the two converted work surfaces already model the discipline —
`conversion-manifest.mjs:372` states it as *"no query changed, no column added,
no capability check touched, no action payload altered"*. Every plan carries that
sentence and the reviewer greps the diff for `select(`, `CAP.`, `.eq(`, `.filter(`.
**Warning sign:** a conversion diff that is not entirely class strings, JSX
structure and imports.

### P5 — Losing a fact that only the card branch carried

**What goes wrong:** the phone card drops a column the desktop table has, or the
reverse.
**Why:** today's six dual-renders each maintain two hand-written lists.
`MemberSpendTable`'s card, for example, drops the `#` rank into the name line and
merges three figures into one meta row — legitimate now, and it must be a
**declared slot** afterwards, not a coincidence.
**How to avoid:** one `columns` array; every column names its slot, `none`
included. `none` is permitted only where the card carries the fact another way
(`DataTable.tsx:98-99`), and D-41.1-13 refuses the money exception.

### P6 — A `sm:` migration that changes the layout instead of the boundary

**What goes wrong:** `sm:grid-cols-3` becomes `md:grid-cols-3` and the tablet
gets three columns in 544 px.
**Why:** §2.3's map is not a rename table for one entry —
`sm:grid-cols-3` becomes **`md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3`**, gaining the middle
step it skipped.
**How to avoid:** read §2.3 per class, not per prefix. Eight of the work group's
21 `sm:` uses are grid-column rules.

### P7 — Declaring a surface converted whose `loading.tsx` is not

**What goes wrong:** criterion 3 is claimed on evidence no gate produced.
**Why:** §1.6 — `loading.tsx` is in no import closure.
**How to avoid:** each page's plan declares its `loading.tsx` explicitly, and the
plan's verification greps it by hand until the closure is extended.
