the primitive it built, delete the debt entries it actually paid, and put a person
in front of every one of those surfaces at three widths.

file. So this task **diffs entries, not counts**, and it is the single declared
owner of every gate edit in the wave (D-41.1-22), which is the only way the
partition rule of D-41.1-15 is satisfiable at all.

Output: a manifest with ten more entries, two gates with three fewer, and a set of
human observation rows.
