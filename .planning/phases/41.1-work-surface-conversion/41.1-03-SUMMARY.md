# Phase 41.1 Plan 03: The Group Tag on Two Gates Summary

Both gates now print the number their criterion is read off — `WORK GROUP
REMAINING = 5` and `WORK GROUP REMAINING = 14` — every entry carries a tag from
a closed vocabulary assigned from its measured importer, and three target
strings that named a surface which does not import the file were re-derived and
corrected.

## What Was Built

### The tag, in both gates

`GROUP_TAGS = ['work', 'public-member-money', 'phase-42', 'exempt']` — a fourth
tuple field, not a fourth list. `verify-tables.mjs`'s `REMAINING` becomes
`[path, reason, target, group]`; `verify-breakpoints.mjs`'s becomes
`[path, count, reason, group]`.

**Why a field and not a list**, written into both docblocks: the reason and the
target already travel with the entry (`verify-routes.mjs:130-152`'s established
form), and a separate list per group puts the group in the *list name* and lets
an entry sit in the wrong one silently. `conversion-manifest.mjs:80` carries a
state column the same way and says in its own docblock that the column is
load-bearing, so this needed no new idea.

**Why the vocabulary is closed.** Free text lets a typo — `work ` with a
trailing space, `Work`, `phase-41.2` instead of `phase-42` — open a fifth group
of one entry that no criterion reads. That is the exact shape of the defect the
tag exists to prevent, so it is refused rather than tolerated.

### `verify-breakpoints.mjs` — the per-group count is deliberately not a total

The count is taken over the **declared** entries, using the **measured** use
count per file. A file carrying `sm:` while on no entry is check B's failure and
belongs to no group: it appears in the undeclared list and in no total here.
Inventing a group for it would let an undeclared file be absorbed into a number
somebody reads a criterion off. That reasoning is written above the loop.

### The class, which is worth more than the three lines

Five wrong reason strings across two gates — three here, two in
`verify-dialogs.mjs` per §5.1 — is a **pattern, not five typos**: every one was
written from the file's **path** rather than from its **importer**, and a path
is not a surface. The rule now written into both gates: *re-derive from the
importer, never from the folder the file sits in*.

Two entries on the breakpoints gate make that a rule rather than a preference,
and they fail the glob test in **opposite directions**:

- **`src/components/media/MediaUpload.tsx`** is not under
  `(public)/events/[slug]/**` at all, and is `public-member-money` — its only
  importer is `(public)/events/[slug]/MediaGallerySection.tsx:8`. A glob-derived
  tag would have missed it and counted it among the fourteen this phase must
  clear.
- **`AssignmentsClient.tsx`** lives *outside* `(work)` — R-WORK-ROUTES keeps
  non-route modules a level out — and is `work`, because
  `(work)/events/[id]/assignments/page.tsx:8` is the only thing that mounts it.

`MediaUpload.tsx` and `MediaReviewGrid.tsx` sit in the **same folder** and carry
**different tags**. That pair is the whole argument for tagging by decision.

## The Caution the Constant Did Not Carry

`verify-breakpoints.mjs`'s `REMAINING` had four rules. It has five now, and the
new one is the one splitting a debt into groups makes necessary:

> **A group count that falls is subject to the same defect as a total that
> falls.** *A debt tracked by a proxy metric is closed by anything that moves
> the metric* (D-41.1-16, four recorded recurrences). Splitting the debt into
> groups **multiplies the proxies rather than fixing them**:
> `WORK GROUP REMAINING = 0` is reached just as well by converting fourteen
> files as by re-tagging one of them `phase-42`. So the per-wave reconciliation
> diffs the **entries** against the tree, never the counts — and a tag that
> changed is a decision to be defended on the line, exactly like a count that
> went down.

That is `T-41.1-08` mitigated where it can actually be read, rather than in a
plan document.

## What a Green Here Does Not Mean

- **The tag is a decision, and no gate can check a decision.** Both gates verify
  a tag is *in the vocabulary*; **neither can verify it is the right one.** A
  work file mistagged `phase-42` passes both gates and silently shrinks the
  number criteria 2 and 4 are read off. The only defence written is procedural —
  the assignment rule in the docblock, the importer named on every entry's own
  reason string so a reader can re-derive it in one command, and D-41.1-16's
  entry-diffing reconciliation.
- **`WORK GROUP REMAINING` counts entries and prefixes, not layouts or cards.**
  H41-3 (a person holding a phone) and H41-1 (three widths) are unmoved by
  anything in this plan.
- **Tagging is not paying.** No entry was added to or removed from either list,
  by this plan's own rules. Both totals are identical to the base commit.
