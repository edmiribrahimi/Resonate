## Written before the diff — the edit route hosts a monotone guard

**Classification: Structured.** These are route files: they resolve access, load
data and render components that are already converted. But **the edit route is the
one work page that reaches the venue reveal**, so its rendering conditions are not
ordinary markup.

**What is opened:** the two route files. **What is not:** the event form, its five
satellites, the reveal panel, the reveal dialog, and every server action they
call — all converted or read in wave 7 by plans that carried their own impact
analyses.

## The three stop conditions, verbatim

1. **`venue_reveal_sent` is monotone.** Work may only make the reveal harder to
   trigger, never easier. **The edit route decides whether the reveal panel is
   rendered at all** — that conditional is behaviour, is quoted before and after,
   and does not move.
2. **A payment reaching `completed` corrects forward.** No status transition,
   refund amount, idempotency key or webhook path is in either closure; asserted
   by grep.
3. **A series progressivo is already on a poster.** No conversion renumbers,
   re-sorts or re-derives one. Both routes pass a night's format, series and
   number to the form; they are passed through untouched.
