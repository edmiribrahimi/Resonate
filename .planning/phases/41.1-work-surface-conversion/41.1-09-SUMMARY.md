## Decisions Made

1. **The three status tiles take no semantic token.** Amber cannot mean *caution* here — `globals.css:161-163` records that the warning semantic is also SunSet's identification colour, so an amber tile cannot say *pending* rather than *this is a SunSet night* by hue alone. And a rejected upload is an ordinary moderation outcome, not a failure, so spending the critical semantic on it is how a red stops meaning anything. The labels were always there; now they do the work alone.
2. **Approve is primary, reject is secondary.** The destructive variant is §11's confirming control *inside a confirmation dialog*. This reject is a single unconfirmed press. Filling it with the critical semantic would shout, and would put the one colour that means *something has gone wrong* on something that has not.
3. **The media item card and the status tiles decline the `Card` primitive, with the reason written next to them.** §8.4 fixes the padding at 24px; a full-bleed thumbnail would be inset by it, and three tiles side by side inside a 390px gutter do not fit at it. Appending a second padding utility to override a primitive's own is the move `DataTable.tsx:476-487` already recorded as not working. This is the same shape of decision `(work)/members/register/page.tsx:343-350` records for its error region.
4. **`ReviewListClient`'s entries and counters *do* take the primitive**, wrapped by the list item and the section rather than replacing them. The entries are a sequence a screen reader should be able to count, and §8.4 owns what a card looks like; neither borrows the other's job.
5. **Both empty states on the media surface were kept distinct rather than merged.** The page's is the server's answer (*nothing was ever pending*); the grid's is the browser's (*you have just decided the last one*). Collapsing them would leave a person unsure whether their last press had landed.
6. **The eleven-pixel debt was paid at the label/data size**, with the accepted consequence — a wider grid, scrolling slightly more — taken as already decided, because it is written into the exemption's own reason at `verify-tables.mjs:115-122`.
