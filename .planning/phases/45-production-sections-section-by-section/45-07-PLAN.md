  <action>
    A pure module. No React, no client, no query.

    **The weights are a declared constant, keyed by `formats.code`**, transcribed from
    `venue-acquisition.md` with the module cited beside each row, and each weight mapped to
    an `AttributeKey` from the vocabulary. Where the module names a criterion the archive
    encodes under a different word, write the mapping and the reason in the same line —
    a mapping that is obvious today is a mapping nobody can check next year.

    **The format whose weights are not declared gets no weights.** `venue-acquisition.md`
    says so in its own table, in words, for one of the four. Its entry is **absent from the
    constant**, and the function returns `{ kind: "weights_not_declared", format }`.
    Inventing weights for it would be `sound-manifesto.md`'s *inventato non è una risposta*
    committed one domain over — writing the brand at the place of whoever owns it.

    **The result is a discriminated union with no bare-number variant**, on
    `PieceDate.tsx:89-94`'s model:

    · `{ kind: "score", value, provenance, askedCount, totalCount }`
    · `{ kind: "weights_not_declared", format }`
    · `{ kind: "not_enough_answered", askedCount, totalCount }` — when too few of that
      format's weighted attributes have been answered for a number to mean anything.
    · `{ kind: "out_of_identity" }` — a real zero, and it is a **different sentence** from
      *not enough answered*. `venue-acquisition.md` keeps an out-of-identity space listed at
      zero rather than deleting it, so a zero must be representable and must be
      distinguishable from an absence.

    **`provenance` on the result is the weakest of the inputs**: `field_verified` only when
    every weighted attribute that contributed was `field_verified`; `derived` otherwise.
    Write the sentence: a computed number is only as verified as its weakest input, and a
    score assembled from one phone call and four public listings is a hypothesis wearing
    the authority of arithmetic.

    **Attributes at `not_asked` do not count as `no`.** They are excluded from the
    denominator and counted in `askedCount` / `totalCount`, because a low score built from
    unanswered questions is *ignorance reported as a negative* — measured context: the
    closest existing attribute is unanswered on more than half the archive.

    **Nothing here reads `real_capacity` as a number when it is null**, and nothing infers
    it from `size_band`. The band is the band; the count is unknown on every record today,
    and inferring one would put a figure in front of a person who would then repeat it.

    Write the module's own limit in its docblock: **the numbers are not comparable across
    formats.** Two results from two formats are two different questions, and any surface
    that sorts them together is answering a question this module did not ask.
  </action>
