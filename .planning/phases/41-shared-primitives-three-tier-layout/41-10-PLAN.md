<objective>
Build the table primitive and convert `/admin/members` onto it — the surface that owns
`MemberTable.tsx`, 1 395 lines, both branches, checkboxes, row actions and wrappers, and
therefore the file plan 41-11's G5 must be proven green against.

Purpose: **six of the seven tables already dual-render**, at two disagreeing breakpoints
— four at `sm` and two at `lg`. DS-09's real content is **consolidation, not
construction** (D-41-17). And the mechanism is a decision, not a detail: **explicitly not**
a `display: block/grid` override on `<table>`/`<tr>`/`<td>`, which destroys native table
semantics and which WebKit still breaks header-and-cell association under — and this
product's primary device is a phone, where every browser is WebKit. Two trees, one hidden
by `display: none`, **neither ever transformed**, which is the property the existing six
already have.

`MemberTable` also carries the two 16px checkboxes §6.4 names by line, and the row
actions that are the **only** site in the product permitted `pointer-fine-only:min-h-9`.

Output: `DataTable` and `Checkbox`; G3 with its exemption written before its first run;
`/admin/members` converted whole; eight surfaces in the manifest.
</objective>
