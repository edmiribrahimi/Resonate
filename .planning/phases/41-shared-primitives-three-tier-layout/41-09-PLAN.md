<objective>
Extract the dialog primitive from the seven byte-identical native shells that already
exist, and convert `/admin/formats` — which owns three of them — onto it in the same
plan. Write G2 here, where its subject first exists.

Purpose: **an eighth shell is not built beside them** (D-41-09). Native `<dialog>` +
`showModal()` supplies Escape, the focus trap and background inertness **by
specification**, which is why the existing shells are right and a hand-rolled overlay is
a step backwards — and why the eleven hand-rolled `fixed inset-0 z-[60]` overlays, none
of which handles any of the three, are the ones that need it most. Two of the seven ask
for the extraction in their own docblocks.

`/admin/formats` is the right first consumer and `RevealVenueDialog` is the wrong one:
the reveal dialog is `venue-secrecy` primary, it is the user interface of a **monotone**
guard, and *"prove it on the hardest correct file"* is not the same as *"convert the most
dangerous surface first."*

Output: `src/components/ui/Dialog.tsx`; `/admin/formats` converted whole, six files;
G2 with a written, shrinking list of what has not converted yet.
</objective>

The initial-focus rule, promoted from a habit to a rule — `RevealVenueDialog.tsx:243-249`:
*"`Cancel` takes the default focus. A confirmation whose Enter key performs the act is a
confirmation that did not ask."* **Two mechanisms exist in the tree for this one intent**
— the `autoFocus` attribute at `RetireFormatDialog.tsx:319` and `RevealVenueDialog.tsx:426`,
and an imperative `.focus()` call. §8.3 names `autofocus`. **Pick one and delete the
other; do not carry both into the primitive.**

The delta from the specimen to §8.3, complete, is `41-PATTERNS.md` §2.1's table. The
load-bearing entries: `z-[60]` is **added** and kept verbatim (it is redundant against
non-top-layer content and meaningful *among* top-layer elements, where `z-index` still
orders); `items-center` becomes `items-end … md:items-center`; the panel loses its
border and takes `rounded-t-2xl md:rounded-2xl bg-surface`; `max-h-[90vh]` becomes
`max-h-[85dvh]` unprefixed; `p-6` becomes three regions; the 32px close button becomes
`IconButton` at 44×44; `text-lg font-bold` becomes the heading role at
`text-base font-semibold`, because 700 does not exist in this system.

The bottom padding is `pb-[calc(1.5rem+var(--nav-inset-block-end))]` — **identical in
computed value** to the verbatim `pb-[calc(1.5rem+5rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]` at
both tiers, because the variable is `0px` from `md` up. The `md:pb-6` half becomes
redundant and is dropped rather than left as a second author.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| dialog → the document behind it | `showModal()` makes the rest of the document inert; a hand-rolled overlay does not, and eleven of those exist |
| a format's catalogue row → the product | the identification colour is data; the moment it becomes a token, a state starts looking like a format |
| a retirement → everything already printed | a progressivo is on a poster; numbering is append-only |
