<objective>
Build the text-entry primitives and convert the three `(auth)` screens onto them. This
closes finding A1 on the surfaces where it is most exposed: the three screens a person
meets before they are anybody.

Purpose: **all 75 form inputs in this tree carry `border border-card-border`**, and
`--card-border` aliases `--line`, which reaches at most **1.39:1** over any ground —
against WCAG 1.4.11's **3:1** for the boundary of an interactive control. Phase 40 wrote
the rule and handed the work forward; D-41-10 named it as this phase's, and says plainly
that the input primitive is **not tidying**. The consequence that makes it sharp: an
input's well is `--sunk` inside a card of `--surface` — **1.04:1**. The fill cannot show
where the control is. **The boundary is the only channel**, and today it does not measure.

The three screens also carry `focus:outline-none` and a primary submit filling with
`--accent` under `text-white` (**2.91:1**) — findings A3 and A2, on `/login`, `/register`
and `/set-password` by name.

Output: `Input`, `Textarea` and `Select` under `src/components/ui/`; three converted
surfaces in the manifest; the `re:sonate` spelling correct where the product says it.

**`Checkbox` and `Switch` are deliberately not here.** Their first consumers are
`MemberTable`'s two `h-4 w-4` boxes (plan 41-10) and `DrinkMenuManager` / `EventForm`
respectively; D-41-04 forbids shipping a primitive in a wave that does not convert a
surface onto it, and this plan's surfaces have neither control.
</objective>

<interfaces>
`PageShell` (`width="focus"`), `Card`, `PageTitle` from plan 41-05; `Button` from plan
41-03. `/login`, `/register` and `/set-password` are three of the four entries on §4's
closed `focus` list.

The class contract, §8.6, verbatim:
```
min-h-11 w-full rounded-xl border border-control bg-sunk px-4 text-sm text-ink
placeholder:text-muted
focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-ink
```
Computed 2026-08-11: boundary `--control` on `--sunk` **7.03**, on `--surface` **6.78**
(both against 1.4.11's 3:1); value `--ink` on `--sunk` **17.02**; placeholder `--muted`
on `--sunk` **7.03**; error text `--sem-crit` on `--surface` **6.99** (all against
1.4.3's 4.5:1).

Label convention, **not a component** (D-41-11): `text-xs font-semibold text-ink-2`
(12.24:1 on `--surface`), and the label is the input's programmatic name via `htmlFor`,
never a sibling `<div>`.

Best specimen for the whole unit — `src/components/venues/CreateVenueModal.tsx:176-200`,
which shows label + control + the `readOnly` variant + the textarea in one block. **Copy
its shape, not its classes:** its boundary is the 1.39:1 one, its label is `font-medium`
(a weight this system does not have), and it has no focus ring at all.
