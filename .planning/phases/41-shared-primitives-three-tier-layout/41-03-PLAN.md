<objective>
Extract the pill family and convert the navigation spine onto it. The navigation gains
its second tier — a persistent side column from 768px up (D-41-02) — and the door keeps
today's form through a wrapper that holds the Phase 42 fence (D-41-21).

Purpose: `MobileNav` is mounted at 13 sites across four route groups and is the file
that makes criterion 1 satisfiable at all — any page converting before it either leaves
an unconverted spine file on a converted page or drags twelve other pages with it.
`StaffNav.tsx:74` is simultaneously four defects in five lines: a ~32px target, `text-white`
on an accent fill (**2.91:1**), `font-medium` (a weight this system does not have) and
`border-card-border` on a control boundary. And RESP-04 has no content until the eight
tabs stop living in a strip that scrolls.

Output: the shared ladder under `src/components/ui/Button.tsx` with **`IconButton`**
exported, and `src/components/ui/Chip.tsx` with **`Chip`** exported; `AppNav` carrying
both tiers; `MobileNav` reduced to a wrapper; `StaffNav` in two forms; the work layout
composing them and carrying a declared, transitional clearance shim.

**Two rungs of the ladder are deliberately not exported here, for the same reason
`Skeleton`, `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `Dialog` and `DataTable` are deferred elsewhere in this
phase.** D-41-04 forbids publishing a primitive in a wave that does not convert a surface
onto it, and this wave converts the spine — which renders navigation entries, a toast and
a format marker, and not one plain button or badge.

- **`Button`** (the plain, labelled form) is declared in plan **41-05**, in the same task
  as `/payment/callback`, whose three `bg-accent … text-white` links are its first render.
- **`Badge`** is declared in plan **41-08**, in the same task as `/admin/members/register`,
  whose stage marks are its first render.

The two rungs that **do** ship here each have a consumer in this wave:
**`IconButton`** is the toast's dismiss control (plan 41-04, same wave), and **`Chip`** is
the eight work tabs on the phone strip (Task 3 of this plan). The shared size and variant
maps live in `Button.tsx` from the start and are not exported until `Button` is, so
41-05 and 41-08 add an export rather than a second ladder.

**The spine has no surface of its own** — converting it shows nobody a half-converted
page (D-41-01). That is why it moves as one unit and why this plan touches six files.
</objective>

border fallback discipline — *"so there is no state in which a chip loses its border"* —
which survives the `--control` migration unchanged; only the class name moves.

The legacy names are **aliases, not survivors** (`globals.css:180-208`):
`--background → --ground`, `--card → --surface`, `--card-border → --line`,
`--foreground → --ink`. **Renaming a legacy utility to its new name is value-identical
and changes no pixel.** That is what makes the door's rendering provably unchanged.
