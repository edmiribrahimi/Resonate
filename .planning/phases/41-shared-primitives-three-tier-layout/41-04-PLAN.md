<objective>
Convert the rest of the shared spine that has a colour: the three toast files and the
format marker. The toast's position stops being a third independent author of `5rem`
and becomes a reader of the variable declared in 41-01.

Purpose: `ToastContainer.tsx:25` hard-codes `calc(5rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom) +
1rem)` — the same assumption the 47 `pb-24`s and the four bottom sheets each write
separately. §3.2 reduces three authors to one. `Toast.tsx` carries **six raw palette
colours** for what are already declared semantics. And §8.3 decided a rule that is
latent today and becomes a silent invisible-notification bug on first use: a native
`<dialog>` paints in the top layer, above the `z-[70]` toast container, so **a dialog
never calls `useToast`** — it reports its outcome inside its own panel. That rule is
written here, before the first dialog is converted, not after somebody loses a message.

**`Skeleton` is deliberately not in this plan.** Its class strings are wrong and its
first consumer is `gallery/loading.tsx`; D-41-04 forbids shipping a primitive in a wave
that does not also convert a surface onto it, so it moves as one piece in plan 41-08.

Output: three toast files reading the variables and the semantic tokens; `FormatMarker`
off its legacy name; and a measured, recorded statement that the remaining spine files
are already clean.
</objective>
