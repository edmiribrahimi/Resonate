<objective>
Round 4 of one guard, and the direction changes.

Three rounds each taught the matcher one more syntactic form, and the next form
always existed: round 1 asserted on the `FOCUS_ROOT` constant and the defect
moved to the render site; round 2 asserted on the outer element and it moved to
the inner element; round 3 asserted on the branch as a brace-balanced region and
it moved to the **shape** of the branch. Four escapes are on record — a ternary,
an `if` without block braces, a `}` inside a string, a concatenation hidden
behind a block comment.

**The diagnosis is not a missing syntax. It is that an unrecognised shape
produces a GREEN.** Every escape in three rounds went through that door.

This plan closes that door by inverting the assertion's direction. Check E stops
hunting for the defect inside a shape it recognises. It asserts instead that the
focus branch has **exactly the one frozen expected shape**, line by line, and
that the `FOCUS_ROOT` declaration has **exactly the one accepted form**. Anything
else — a ternary, a brace-less `if`, a brace inside a string, a concatenation, a
fragment, a nested conditional, an extracted helper, a reordered attribute, or
any refactor nobody has imagined yet — produces a refusal or a failure. The gate
then does not need to anticipate syntaxes: everything that is not the expected
shape fails.

**This plan does not add four regex cases for the four observed escapes.** The
four escapes are the evidence that the hunting direction cannot be completed by
enumeration. A plan that patched them one by one would be round 4 of the same
mistake.

**The cost, accepted here and not discovered later.** A gate that refuses every
unanticipated shape will also refuse a *legitimate* refactor of that branch until
someone updates the frozen shape. That is the correct direction — it fails closed
rather than open — but whoever touches `PageShell.tsx` in 41.1 will meet a red
suite and must be able to find out why in seconds. So the refusal message is part
of the deliverable, not decoration: it says what shape was expected, what was
found, where the two first differ, and what to do about it. And it distinguishes,
in its own first line, a refusal caused by a legitimate refactor from a refusal
caused by a gate that could not read what it needs.

Purpose: RESP-01 and RESP-02's code regression (CR-01) is closed and has stayed
closed since round 1. What has never worked is anything that would notice it
coming back. **Neither requirement closes here, and neither does DS-07, DS-08,
DS-09, RESP-03 or RESP-04.**

Output: `scripts/verify-conversion.mjs` only. No product file is modified by this
plan. `src/components/ui/PageShell.tsx` is mutated **transiently** by the
verification steps and restored byte-for-byte, with the restore asserted rather
than assumed.
