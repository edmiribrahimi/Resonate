<objective>
Plan 41-26 inverted the guard. This plan tries to break it.

**Why this is a plan of its own and not the last section of 41-26.** Three rounds
have shipped gates that passed their own mutation proofs and were open anyway.
Every one of those proofs was written by the person who had just written the fix,
at the end of a long task, against the shapes that person had in mind — which are
precisely the shapes the fix already handles. The escape has never once been a
shape the author considered. So the adversarial step gets its own context window
and its own commit, and its job is not to confirm the fix: **its job is to find a
green.**

**The acceptance criterion is adversarial, not enumerative.** You are not asked to
prove that four named shapes redden. You are asked to try a set of deliberately
unusual rewrites of the focus branch — including at least three you invent, that
appear nowhere in this plan — and to establish that **none of them produces a
tick**. Then you record how many you tried and state plainly, in one sentence,
whether any produced a green.

An enumerated list is the shape that failed three times. A challenge to find a
green is the shape that catches the fifth form.

**If you find a green, that is a success of this plan, not a failure of it.** The
resolution is never to teach the matcher the shape that escaped — that is round 1,
2 and 3 in order. The resolution is to strengthen the frozen-shape assertion so
the whole class cannot escape, and then run the entire campaign again from the
start.

**Three findings of round 3 are open and no plan in this round takes them**, so
they are named here rather than left to be inferred from their absence: **WR-02**
(`verify-dialogs.mjs`'s rung family narrower than the sentence its report prints,
with a measured delta of zero occurrences on this tree) and **WR-04**
(`FULL_BLEED_VIEWER`'s exemption premise trusted rather than measured) — both
detailed in 41-28's objective, which edits that file and deliberately does not
touch them — and **WR-05** (this file's header carrying three measured line
numbers that nothing re-measures), detailed in 41-26's objective. This campaign
hunts one guard; it does not hunt those, and a campaign that reported clean would
otherwise read as a clean bill for the file.

Purpose: RESP-01 and RESP-02's guard has been declared closed and found open three
times. **Neither requirement closes here**, and neither does DS-07, DS-08, DS-09,
RESP-03 or RESP-04.

Output: a campaign record. `scripts/verify-conversion.mjs` is modified **only if
the campaign finds a green**; on a clean campaign this plan changes no file and
its deliverable is the record.
</objective>

    green, and a green is the thing you are looking for.

    it as a refusal on a legitimate refactor, not as a defect. If a rewrite refuses and
    the branch carries the defect, check the marker: `SHAPE CHANGED` is acceptable,
    `GATE CANNOT READ` on a shape that is plainly readable means the gate is blindfolded
