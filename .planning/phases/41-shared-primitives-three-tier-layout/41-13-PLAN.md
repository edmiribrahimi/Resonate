<objective>
Close **CR-01**, the one blocking gap in `41-VERIFICATION.md`: `PageShell width="focus"`
reserves navigation clearance on the four surfaces that mount no navigation at all.

Purpose: `/login`, `/register`, `/set-password` and `/payment/callback` are §4's closed
focus list. `find src/app -name layout.tsx` returns exactly two files — the root layout and
the `(work)` one — and neither covers `(auth)` or `(public)/payment`; no file under either
directory imports `AppNav` or `MobileNav`. So on those four screens
`--nav-inset-inline-start` resolves to its global value of 14rem from 768px up and
`--nav-inset-block-end` to 5rem plus the device safe-area below it, and the shell reserves
both. Measured consequence: at or above 768px the leading padding is 248px against a
trailing 24px, so a `max-w-sm` card inside `justify-center` sits **112px right of the
viewport centre** — the exact number `PageShell`'s own docblock cites as the failure mode
it was written to avoid. Below 768px the card carries **~96px of bottom padding** under
which there is no bar.

This is a regression, not an inherited state: at `a3f61ad` `/login` was centred and
`/payment/callback` was centred. It reaches the product's front door and the screen that
reports the outcome of a payment, and **no gate can see it** — G4 check D asks only whether
the shell declares §4's three maxima and whether a page wrote one of its own.

Output: the focus form loses the clearance; the default and wide forms keep it unchanged;
and the observation nobody has yet made is written down as owed.

**What this does not close.** DS-07, DS-08, DS-09 and RESP-01…RESP-04 all remain PARTIAL.
RESP-01 closes only after phase 41.2. This plan removes a confirmed failure from four of
the eight surfaces this phase declares; it does not turn a partial requirement whole, and
it produces no human observation — it produces a written procedure for one.
</objective>
