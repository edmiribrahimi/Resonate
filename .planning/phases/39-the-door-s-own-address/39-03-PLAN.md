<constraints>
## The decision this plan states out loud rather than absorbing

**Pass BOTH `capabilities` and `liveAssignmentCapabilities`, and write the widening down.**

The middleware admits the door on **role or live assignment**, and the page guard repeats
that predicate. If only `capabilities` crossed the boundary, a member of staff rostered to
tonight's door — who holds `door.operate` **by assignment and by nothing else**, `staff`
being one of the six declared refusals of the role — would still be drawn **no** Check-in
tab. That is the same safe direction the divergence already had, so it would not be a
regression; but it would close half of D-39-06 and leave the other half open under a heading
that says "closed".

Passing both makes the nav match the server exactly. It is also **wider**, and the width is
this: `liveAssignmentCapabilities` is coarse and **does not name a night** — it is *"wider
than the real permission, always and by construction"* — so somebody assigned to a
**different** night is drawn a tab, the middleware admits them, the page admits them, and
they **do not find their night in the list**. No refusal, no promise broken, which is the
asymmetry `checkin-offline.md` optimises for. **That paragraph belongs in the code**, not
only in this plan.

## This is a visibility change, and nothing else

`access-gating.md`, gate *coerenza navigazione/permessi*: *"Nascondere un link non è
proteggere una rotta: ogni voce nascosta deve avere il suo controllo lato server."* The
server-side control here is the guard in `DoorSurface.tsx`, and the real boundary on the
door's **data** is `requireDoorOperator({ partyId })` in the three door Route Handlers,
which write with the service client and see no policy. **Neither is touched by this plan**,
and the summary must say so with the assertion that proves it.

## The public-surface rule, which must be asserted rather than assumed

Eight of the thirteen mount sites are public pages. For an anonymous visitor
`getAccessContext()` returns `ANONYMOUS_CONTEXT` with an empty capability set, so the
Check-in entry is filtered out exactly as it is today. **No verdict on a public surface
changes** — but CAP-05 criterion 4 forbids a verdict change on a public surface, so this
plan states it explicitly and proves it by reasoning from the empty set rather than by
hoping.
